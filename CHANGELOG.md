# Changelog

Every change a product sees, newest first. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org); before 1.0 a minor version may break.

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

The first release.

- 107 components, one file each: `src/components/<name>.tsx`, with `<name>.stylex.ts` where it defines markers or variables; data-table, data-grid and markdown are folders. The primitives run from accordion to tooltip, with markdown, snippet and data-grid among them.
- NativeSelect, the browser's own select in the field's box (`NativeSelectOption`, `NativeSelectOptGroup`), and SortableList, a list put in order by hand by each item's handle, from the pointer, a finger or the keyboard (`SortableListItem`, `SortableListHandle`, on UI SDK's `createReorder`). Heading takes `ref` and `tabIndex={-1}`, a place for focus when the control that had it goes.
- Four blocks: app-shell-01, connect-api-key-01, inspector-01, share-dialog-01. What the components and blocks share is in `lib/`, `hooks/` and `styles/`.
- The AI components and patterns, each installable on its own. The components: message, bubble, message-scroller, marker, attachment, questionnaire, trace and code-block. The patterns, built on them: chain-of-thought, chat, confirmation, context, inline-citation, model-selector, prompt-input, reasoning, sources, stack-trace, suggestion, terminal, test-results and tool.
- A visible label is children or a label part, a control with no visible text takes `aria-label` and never a `label` prop, and the words a component shows or says itself and lets you replace end in `Text`. A hidden name for a part you cannot reach ends in `Label` (`thumbLabels`, `triggerLabel`). What changed:

- Tokens are `--damsko-*` CSS variables and StyleX constants by category (`colors`, `space`, `radius`, `shadow`, `font`, `duration`, `size`, `media`), from `@damsko/tokens` and `@damsko/lib`. Icons are one house set of Hugeicons drawings, named by shape, in `lib/icons.ts`: the drawings Damsko's parts use and the shapes products share. Any other Hugeicons drawing a product imports from `@hugeicons/core-free-icons` and draws through the same `Icon`, marked with `directional` when it points along the line.
- A shadcn registry at `https://damsko.joingrasp.com/r/{name}.json`: add it to `components.json` as `@damsko` and install with the stock CLI (`pnpm dlx shadcn@4.23.0 add @damsko/button`). Every file lands byte for byte at its own path under `src/` (`registry:file`, `~/src/…` targets, packages by name only), so `components.json` needs `baseColor: ""` and no other setting changes.
- The primitives' few built-in words come from `I18nProvider`. Its catalogue may be built while rendering, from a translation library: the provider keeps the words it has until `locale`, now required, or a string changes. The action hook's words are the part `action` (was `useAction`, which the React Compiler read as a hook).
- `@damsko/fonts`: Geist, Geist Mono and Geist Serif under the tokens' names, as six subset files and their licence, one sheet per family (`styles/fonts/geist.css`, `geist-serif.css`, `geist-mono.css`) and `styles/fonts.css` for the three. The files arrive as base64 text and `fonts/unpack.mjs` unpacks them, once, after an install: the product commits them, and `--check` fails while one is packed, missing or altered. Each font token names one family; a product that serves Geist itself leaves that sheet out and sets `--damsko-font-sans` to its own family, so one Geist loads.
- Marker takes `color`, its words and rules in one of the brand's colours (`primary`, `mint`, `pine`, `lavender`, `success`, `warning`, `destructive`, `info`; the muted `default` is unchanged), and `decorative={false}` on a separator, read as a separator named by its words, so a line such as where the unread messages start needs no wrapper.
- `@damsko/tokens` brings `styles/base.css`, an optional reset for a new app, in the cascade layer `base`; it is not for an app beside Tailwind, and no component leans on it.
- `@damsko/all` is every item by name, with no file of its own: one command installs or updates them all (`shadcn add @damsko/all --overwrite`). Each item's npm packages are exact and minimal, held to an install of that item alone.
- Every registry item says its version, commit and licence in `meta`, and every release is served at `/r/<tag>/{name}.json`, the address a product pins, from the archive its tag's release carries (`registry-<tag>.tgz` and its checksum).
- The toolchain is the product repository's: pnpm, Vite+ with Oxfmt, Oxlint and Ultracite, TypeScript 7 with the product's strict flags, Vitest.
- Looks and defaults brought to the original's: the sidebar's rows and group labels take a plain 6px corner (`sidebar-row-radius`, 10px for an `lg` row, `sidebar-row-radius-lg`) and its sheet on a narrow window the page card's 12px; a Button in a Dialog, AlertDialog, Sheet or Drawer footer that names no size is `sm`, as its Close; a `default` PromptInputBanner pads 8px at its sides, stands its words 8px after its mark, keeps no room for an action it does not have and is no live region; a model's name in the ModelSelector chip is in ink; a ToggleGroup's items that do not fit its box go on to another row. New: Button `variant="success"` (the yes that approves or confirms, ConfirmationAction's default), `TabsList fullWidth`, `TabsTrigger icon`, `PromptInputTools align="end"`, `ItemGroup stack`, `Tooltip toggleable`, `CodeBlockContent maxLines={false}` drawing every line, chart rows kept by their place, and sixteen icons from the original's set (`AppWindowIcon`, `CableIcon`, `CircleHelpIcon`, `ArrowLeftRightIcon`, `ContrastIcon`, `LaptopIcon`, `TabletIcon`, `SmartphoneIcon`, `ZapIcon`, `ScanIcon`, `ZoomInIcon`, `ZoomOutIcon`, `SplitIcon`, `ScaleIcon`, `FileInvoiceIcon`, `Minimize2Icon`).
- Markdown is read a block at a time by micromark and its unfinished end made whole by remend (a new dependency): a finished block is read and drawn once, an open fence is never parsed, a block being written is read again at most a fifth of the time, and each new word fades in while it streams (none with reduced motion, none in a link). A link of its own may lead to `tel:` and a path on this site, as `safeHref` now allows everywhere. `softBreaks` is on or off (`"auto"` is gone); a table draws and reads at most 1,000 rows, saying how many more, and draws what follows it; lists and quotes nested past 32 are drawn 32 deep (a quote with no space after its `>`, at most 64); a paragraph with more than 1,000 marks, or a block with more than 5,000, is drawn as words, and a block with a word of more than 1,000 characters draws its bare addresses as words; a list past 1,000 lines goes on as another at its next item; a paragraph past 1,000 lines goes on as another; `<details>` sections are read as one block, their body as blocks of their own.
- Markdown's tables wrap their words, names included, so a table of sentences fits the answer's column on a phone too; a column set to the right stays on one line, code in a cell may break, and each column keeps the narrowest a column may be (`table-column-min`), so only a table that cannot fit scrolls sideways. While written, a table's even columns are each at least that wide (Table's `streaming`). TableHead takes `wrap`, TableHead and TableCell take `dir`. Each paragraph, heading, list item, quote and cell takes its words' direction (`dir="auto"`). GitHub's alerts (`> [!NOTE]`, `[!TIP]`, `[!IMPORTANT]`, `[!WARNING]`, `[!CAUTION]`) are Alerts, titled by `markdown.alerts` in the I18nProvider. A screen reader hears the blocks finished before an answer still being written is first drawn, and nothing of an answer drawn whole. A note's mark stands within its line whatever the app's reset.
- StatGroup reads Stats made with `map` beside ones written out, as its cells in order; before, it kept only the ones written out. A card that is `fullHeight` in a row sized by its cards shows all its CardContent, its footer at the foot; before, the content was squeezed to nothing and clipped. Six icons: `HistoryIcon`, `HourglassIcon`, `MicOffIcon`, `PhoneIcon`, `PhoneOffIcon`, `UnplugIcon`.
- CodeBlock is coloured by `@pierre/highlights` (a new dependency, 0.5.2): lexers written by hand and compiled to WebAssembly, read on the page and loaded the first time a block is drawn (one module of 161 KB compressed, every language in it; Prism's worker was 24 KB). Prism, its worker, the reading queue and its time limits are gone: no text can hold the page (the string of 50,000 characters that held Prism for seconds is read in under a millisecond), and while code is written each line is read once and coloured as its newline comes. A page's Content Security Policy needs `'wasm-unsafe-eval'` in `script-src` and nothing in `worker-src`; without it the code is plain. A line of 250 characters or more is drawn as its text, a block past 5,000 coloured runs is plain (counted before as all runs), and any language the lexers know is coloured, named as it is written. The Many blocks example is gone with the queue it showed.
- Chart draws stacked bars, a line at a value, and bars that can be pressed. ChartBars that share a `stackId` stack, each series in a chart tone by its place in its stack, named in ChartLegend and the tooltip beside a square of its tone. `ChartReferenceLine` draws a dashed line across the drawing at a `y` (a budget, a limit) or up it at an `x`, its name (children) at its end, and the axis grows to hold it. `onRowClick` on ChartContainer makes each point along the bottom a button, reached by Tab and the arrow keys and pressed with Enter or Space, named by its point and values; `selectedIndex` marks the pressed one and steps the other bars back. A chart without these is drawn as before.
