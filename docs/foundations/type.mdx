## Sizes

Sizes of words and their line heights are in rem, so they follow the reader's text size, as do the width of a column of words and the breakpoints. Controls, icons, marks, space, corners and edges are in px and follow the page's zoom: an icon or a tick never outgrows the control that holds it. A field's words on a phone are `max(16px, 1rem)`: never under 16px, and still the reader's size above it.
