## What takes which step

One scale, named by size as shadcn names it. `radius` is the base, 20px; `radius-lg`, `radius-xl` and `radius-2xl` are made from it, so they follow it, and the steps under them are fixed. A part takes a step, never the base, and never a radius of its own.

| Step | Value | What takes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `radius-xs` | 2px | A thing too thin for `radius-sm`: an 8px swatch, a highlight's edge. |
| `radius-sm` | 6px | A mark under 24px tall: a key, inline code, the box of a checkbox, a square tile at 24px. |
| `radius-md` | 12px | The page's card beside an inset sidebar: the app's main surface, a plain round corner. It holds no sidebar rows, so it does not nest round them. |
| `radius-lg` | the base less 4px, so 16px | A row: a menu or list item, an Item, a tree or sidebar row, a trigger that runs the width, in a card too. A container keeps 4px round its rows, so a row's corner is the container's less that room. |
| `radius-xl` | the base, 20px | Controls and containers: buttons, fields, cards, menus, panels, a square tile. |
| `radius-2xl` | the base plus 4px, so 24px | A surface that nests rows 8px in: a floating sidebar, a row's 16px plus the 8px it keeps round its rows. |
| `radius-full` | 999px | A pill: a chip, a badge, the track of a switch, a slider or a progress bar. |

Every step is capped at half the height of what takes it, so a small control becomes a pill and a row never rounds past its own ends.

`radius-circle` is 50%, for an avatar or a dot. It stands beside `radius-full` because a percentage follows its box and a length does not: half of each side, so it grows with the box and a dot can turn into a square on the way to an error.

The Prompt input's card keeps its own token, `prompt-input-radius`: 24px, a button's corner at its size (16px) plus the footer's 8px. It is not `radius-2xl` because the bands above and under the card tuck behind it by the same length, and because it stays 24px in a browser that draws no squircle, where the scale's corners are smaller.

## Nesting

An outer corner is the inner corner plus the room between them, and an inner corner is the outer one less that room. A row in a menu is 20px less 4px; a floating sidebar is 16px plus 8px; a segment is its track's corner less the track's padding.

## The shape

`radius-shape` is the system's corner shape, a squircle: every corner on the scale writes it as `corner-shape` after its radius, so the curve eases into the edge. A pill and a circle leave it out: they are round by their radius alone, and the squircle would draw a rounded rectangle.
