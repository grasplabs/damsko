## On their own

The label is quiet, the value leads, and the change says in words and colour how it moved.

<Stage path="stat/single" label="On their own" />

## In a group

`StatGroup` reads them as one row: one box, a hairline between each, at most two in a row on a tablet or a phone.

<Stage path="stat/grid" label="In a group" />

The group takes its figures as `items`, or as `Stat` children.

<Stage path="stat/children" label="Stats as children" />

## With its trend

`chart` takes a small chart under the figures: a `ChartSparkline` at its tile size. With the spark's `onPoint`, the figure can follow the week under the pointer or the arrow keys.

<Stage path="chart/spark-tile" label="With its trend" />

## Loading

Skeletons hold the value's room, so nothing moves when the figures arrive.

<Stage path="stat/loading" label="Loading" />

## Every state

Nothing in it is pressed, so its states are its variants, no change, an icon, and loading.

<Stage path="stat/states" label="Every state" />
