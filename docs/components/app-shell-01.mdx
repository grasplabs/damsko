A block is a ready-made composition of components that a product installs, then owns and edits. It is not a primitive.

## Basic usage

`sidebar` takes the product's Sidebar parts; the page stands beside it as a card. Across the card's top, the header holds the sidebar's trigger (Cmd+B or Ctrl+B fold it too), where the page is and its `actions`. Where the page is: `breadcrumb` is the steps before it, BreadcrumbItems alone, and `title` the page; the shell writes the slash between every two. With `onRename` a press on the page's name, Enter or F2 renames it in place. The page scrolls under the header.

## A panel at the end

`panel` holds one Side panel at the page's end, seated unframed. Drag its handle or move it with the arrow keys; the header's panel button hides and shows it. On a narrow window it opens as a sheet from the end, which holds Tab inside and gives focus back to the button when it closes. Keep it with `panelOpen` and `onPanelOpenChange`.

<Stage path="app-shell-01/panel" label="A panel at the end" />

## A banner

`banner` stands above the header, across the page: a notice for the whole product, on every page.

<Stage path="app-shell-01/banner" label="A banner" />

## Levels

An area with a navigation of its own takes the sidebar while the person is in it: `sidebar` holds Sidebar's SidebarLevels, and the address says which level is shown. Under `settings/` here it is Settings' own list, with the way back to the page that was left.

<Stage path="app-shell-01/levels" label="Levels" />

## On a phone

Under the lg breakpoint the sidebar is Sidebar's own sheet from the start, opened by the same trigger; following a link in it closes it.
