## Files to send

An `AttachmentGroup` lists the files; each `Attachment` holds an `AttachmentMedia`, an `AttachmentContent` with its `AttachmentTitle` and `AttachmentDescription` (the size, through `formatFileSize`), and `AttachmentActions`. Name each `AttachmentAction` for its file. More files than room scroll sideways.

<Stage path="attachment/basic" label="Files to send" />

## Pictures and marks

`AttachmentMedia` with `src` shows the picture as itself. A file with none, or whose picture will not load, wears the mark for its `type`. With no actions, nothing takes a file off.

<Stage path="attachment/previews" label="Pictures and marks" />

## Pictures from a chat

A picture loads only from an address that cannot tell another site who looked: one written into the address (`data:` of a picture or video type, never SVG), one the page made (`blob:`) or a path on this site; an `http(s)` address loads only with `allowRemote`, for a source you trust.

<Stage path="attachment/untrusted" label="Pictures from a chat" />

## Your own mark

Put your own mark for a kind of file in the `AttachmentMedia`, in place of the one for its media type.

<Stage path="attachment/parts" label="Your own mark" />

## On its way

`state` says where the file is: `idle`, `uploading`, `processing`, `error` and `done`. Say why it failed in its description, so colour is not the only sign.

<Stage path="attachment/states" label="On its way" />

## Sizes

`size` is `default`, `sm` or `xs`.

<Stage path="attachment/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## The whole card as a press

`AttachmentTrigger` covers the card behind its actions: a link to the file, or a Dialog's trigger. Upright (`orientation="vertical"`), the picture fills the card's width.

<Stage path="attachment/trigger" label="The whole card as a press" />
