## By maker

A search field across the top; down the side, a column of the makers' logos (`ModelSelectorMakers`, each a `ModelSelectorMaker`); beside it, the chosen maker's models under its name (`ModelSelectorGroup`, its `value` the maker's). The panel opens on the chosen model's maker. Typing searches every maker. The models, makers and logos are yours; `ModelSelector` holds the chosen model (`value` and `onValueChange`). Picking closes the panel, and the chip then names the model.

<Stage path="model-selector/basic" label="By maker" />

## Favourites

With `favorites`, a star heads the column and lists them. A row's `ModelSelectorFavorite` adds its model or takes it out without picking it, through `onFavoritesChange`; from the search, the arrow toward the line's end does the same for the highlighted model. The change is said. Leave `favorites` out for a product without them.

<Stage path="model-selector/favorites" label="Favourites" />

## Effort

`ModelSelectorEffort` at the panel's foot takes the chosen model's levels and shows each by name as one row of segments, the chosen one raised (`effort` and `onEffortChange`). With an effort to set, picking a model keeps the panel open, so both are chosen in one opening. A model with one level or none has no row: it folds away. The chip shows the maker's logo and the level in a word, and says the model in its `tooltip`.

<Stage path="model-selector/effort" label="Effort" />

This is the one effort control for a chat: a Slider's `levels` stays for a level set on its own, not beside a model.

## In a prompt input

Among a [Prompt input](/components/prompt-input)'s tools, opening upwards with `side="top"`.
