## By maker

The models are yours: group them by maker with `ModelSelectorGroup`, each with its maker's logo (`ModelSelectorLogo`, an IconData of the product's). `ModelSelectorList` holds the chosen model (`value` and `onValueChange`). Typing narrows the list; the arrows and Enter pick; the trigger then names the model picked.

<Stage path="model-selector/basic" label="By maker" />

## With a footer

`footer` on `ModelSelectorContent` holds what the product adds under the list, such as how hard the model thinks.

<Stage path="model-selector/footer" label="With a footer" />

## Model and effort

Two menus under a message field, as shadcn's pattern has them: the model, searched, and how hard it thinks, a Dropdown menu of three beside it.
