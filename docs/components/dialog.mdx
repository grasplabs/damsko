## With a title and description

A title that names the task, a sentence under it, and the actions that finish it. The trigger is a plain wrapper: `asChild` hands its behaviour to a Button. Cancel is a `DialogClose`, which draws an outline Button beside the action; the action is a `default` Button whose `onClick` returns a Promise and closes the dialog once its work is done. While it works, the dialog holds: its `onOpenChange` cancels the close.

## Long body

When the body holds more than the window, only the body scrolls.

<Stage path="dialog/long-body" label="Long body" />

## Full

`size="full"` on the content opens a chart or a table in full, up to `dialog-width-full` (64rem), never closer than `dialog-gutter` to the window's edges. The body scrolls inside, and fades at its foot while there is more. On a phone it is the same sheet as any dialog.

<Stage path="dialog/full" label="Full" />

## On a phone

Under 640px it is a sheet along the bottom, and each Button in its footer takes an equal share of the line, under the thumb. Nothing to set: the window's width decides.

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="dialog"
  always={[
    "A title that says what the dialog does, in sentence case: Publish the report.",
    "Cancel first, then the one action the dialog is for, last.",
    "An `errorText` on the action's Button that says plainly what did not happen: Could not publish.",
    "A task that needs the page out of the way, finished by its actions, on a phone too: under 640px it is a sheet along the bottom.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "A dialog opened from inside another dialog.",
    "A footer action that keeps the dialog open: a step inside the task belongs in the body.",
    "A link in the footer that leaves the page.",
    "A small task beside the control that opened it, with the page in reach: use Popover.",
    "Details or a side task that keeps the page in view: use Sheet.",
    "A panel on a phone that rests at more than one height or is dragged: use Drawer.",
    "One question before a grave step that cannot be undone: use Alert dialog.",
  ]}
