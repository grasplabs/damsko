## Basic usage

A title that says what it is, and a sentence under it.

<Stage path="card/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Variants

`default` is the usual card, lifted off the page. `outline` is a quiet panel that lies in it.

<Stage path="card/grounds" label="Variants" />

## Padding

16px inside for a grid of many cards, 24px for one that stands alone, none for rows that run to the edge.

<Stage path="card/padding" label="Padding" />

## In a list

Each card an `li` in a `ul` (`asChild`), its title a heading. A long name wraps and the card grows.

<Stage path="card/list" label="In a list" />

## A card that is one link

`CardLink` makes the whole card one link; hovered or focused, its media dims and its title is underlined. Put no other control inside.

<Stage path="card/link" label="A card that is one link" />

## Header, action and footer

The parts: `CardHeader` holds the `CardTitle` and `CardDescription`, with a `CardAction` at its end beside both; `CardFooter` puts its buttons or a total in a row at the foot (`justify` to stand them at the end or apart). A card made of parts stands them 16px apart.

<Stage path="card/parts" label="Header, action and footer" />

## A part of a page

With a `CardContent`, the parts carry the room themselves: the header, what it holds and a line at the foot under a hairline. Drawn as a `section` (`asChild`), the card is a landmark named by its title.

<Stage path="card/section" label="A part of a page" />

A card has no `loading` prop: while its words are coming, put a Skeleton in the CardHeader, as [Loading](/patterns/loading#a-card-whose-words-are-coming) shows.

## Flush

`CardContent flush`: a table or a list runs edge to edge under a hairline, and the header and the footer line up with its first column.

<Stage path="card/flush" label="Flush" />

## As tall as its cell

`fullHeight` makes the card as tall as its grid cell: its CardContent takes the room the header and the footer leave, so the footer stands at the foot. In a row as tall as its tallest card, each shows what it holds whole; in a cell of a set height, what runs past the foot is clipped.

<Stage path="card/full-height" label="As tall as its cell" />

## In a narrow card

When the card is narrow, the header's actions take the line under the title, never past the edge.

<Stage path="card/members" label="In a narrow card" />

## Small

`size="sm"` for a settings card: the header at text-sm, the same room above the first row with or without a sentence.

<Stage path="card/compact" label="Small" />

## Without a title

One card on a page whose title names it: no header, `aria-label` names it, the rows flush to the top.

<Stage path="card/no-title" label="Without a title" />

## Title above the card

A group of settings: the title stands on the page and the card holds the rows alone. A composition, not a part: a `section` named by its `SectionHeader`, then the `Card`.

<Stage path="card/grouped" label="Title above the card" />
