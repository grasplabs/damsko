## From the end

Along the end, the right in English: details and side tasks beside the page. The trigger is a plain wrapper: `asChild` hands its behaviour to a Button. Close is a `SheetClose`, which draws an outline Button beside the action; the action is a `default` Button whose `onClick` returns a Promise and closes the sheet once its work is done.

<Stage path="sheet/basic" live label="From the end" />

## From the start

`side="start"` on the content, for places to go. Both sides follow the reading direction. A sheet opens along the start or the end only: a panel that rises from the bottom is a Drawer.

<Stage path="sheet/start" label="From the start" />

## Wide

`size="lg"` on the content holds a layout of its own. A sheet has three widths, `default`, `lg` and `full`, and no `xl`: a fourth name would only be a second one for `lg`.

<Stage path="sheet/wide" label="Wide" />

## Full

`size="full"` on the content opens a chart or a table in full beside the page, up to `sheet-width-full` (64rem). The body scrolls inside, and fades at its foot while there is more. On a phone it is the full sheet, and each Button in its footer takes an equal share of the line.

<Stage path="sheet/full" label="Full" />

## Long body

When the body holds more than the window, only the body scrolls.

<Stage path="sheet/long-body" label="Long body" />
