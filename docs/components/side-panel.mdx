## Basic usage

Scroll it: the bar stays, gains its hairline, and takes the views as icons. The views are tabs: `defaultValue` or `value` on the SidePanel, and one SidePanelContent per view. Whole screens: [the inspector pattern](/patterns/inspector).

<Stage path="side-panel/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Views alone

Without a title, the views are the head: they stand at the panel's top with their words, never fold, and stay while the content scrolls. The SidePanel's `aria-label` names the panel, and `viewsLabel` the views. For a side panel of the page's own thing, a note's links and comments, where a title would only repeat the page's.

<Stage path="side-panel/views-only" label="Views alone" />

## Sections

Each section is a quiet title, one action at the end of its row and one card under it. A `description` explains the title in a tooltip, under a dashed line. Empty, the card is one row that adds the first: a `SidePanelSectionAdd`, its words what it adds, named by the section's title.

<Stage path="side-panel/section" label="Sections" />

## Long text

SidePanelText holds a long text in a card (an agent's instructions, a note): it stands short, its words fade at its foot, and the button in its corner opens it to its full height and closes it again.

<Stage path="side-panel/text" label="Long text" />

## Cover

`cover` stands behind the head of the one thing a page is about, reaching up behind the bar: a faint picture or texture of the product's own. With no bar above the head (no actions, no close, no views), it starts at the head's own top.

<Stage path="side-panel/cover" label="Cover" />
