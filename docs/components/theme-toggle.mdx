## Basic usage

Controlled: give it the `mode` and an `onModeChange` that applies it (`data-mode` where the product wants it) and keeps it. It reads and writes no storage itself.

<Stage path="theme-toggle/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Icon button

`variant="icon"` where there is no room: one button that cycles, at 32 or 28px.

<Stage path="theme-toggle/icon" label="Icon button" />

## Picker

`variant="picker"` on a settings page. The themes under the modes are the product's, each with its name (`themes`), chosen with `theme` and `onThemeChange`; their ids are the stylesheet's (`themeIds`).

<Stage path="theme-toggle/picker" label="Picker" />

## Small tiles

`tileSize="default"` keeps each tile at its own width, from the row's start, for a settings column.

## When to use it

A person chooses light, dark or the system's look, kept with the product's settings: the product holds the choice, applies it, keeps it and sets it before the page paints. Not a look for only the part it sits in, and not any other setting that turns on or off: use a [Switch](/components/switch). Never a theme change with no way back to the system's setting.
