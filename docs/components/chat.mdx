## The thread

Chat is the thread of a conversation with the assistant. Each turn is a `ChatMessage`, a Message in a Message scroller row; the person's turn is the scroll anchor, so a new question settles near the top and the reply streams in under it, followed while you stay at the end. The field under it is a Prompt input.

<Stage path="chat/demo" label="The thread" />

## Before the first turn

`ChatEmptyState` stands in the thread until the first turn: a mark, a title and a sentence.

<Stage path="chat/empty" label="Before the first turn" />

## A checkpoint

A checkpoint is a point the person can take the conversation back to, after a turn that changed files or settings. It is not a component: put a separator Marker at the end of that turn, with its words and a small outline Button in the middle of the hairline, and a Tooltip on the button for what restoring drops. Restore keeps that turn and drops every turn after it; with nothing after it, hold the button out of use (`focusableWhenDisabled`) so its tooltip can say why.
