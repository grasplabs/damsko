## Basic usage

`CollapsibleTrigger` says what opens; `CollapsibleContent` holds the detail most people can skip.

<Stage path="collapsible/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Several

`variant="raised"` for panels that stand on their own; each opens apart from the others.

<Stage path="collapsible/several" label="Several" />

## Fit

With `fitContent`, the trigger is only as wide as its words, the chevron beside them: one quiet line that opens.

<Stage path="collapsible/fit" label="Fit" />

## Disabled

The trigger stays focusable and does nothing.

<Stage path="collapsible/disabled" label="Disabled" />
