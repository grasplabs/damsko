## A settings page

The serif title and a card per topic in a 720px column in the page's middle, its margins equal, and a table under them in the same column, its edges on the cards' edges.

<Stage path="container/settings" label="A settings page" />

## Settings width

`size="sm"` makes the column 640px, and a table on a settings page stays in it. `size="lg"` makes it 1024px, for a page of wider things. `ContainerBleed` is for what needs more room than the column, a wide table or a chart on a page of wider things, and runs the page's whole width. `align="start"` stands any column at the page's start instead, with all the room past it at its end.
