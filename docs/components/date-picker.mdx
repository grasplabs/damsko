## Basic usage

Type a date in the page's language, or open the calendar with the button at the field's end; a day chosen closes it. A typed date is read when you press Enter or leave the field, then written out; text that is no date stays, marked wrong. The form gets an ISO date under `name`.

<Stage path="date-picker/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Range

`mode="range"` gives a field for the start and one for the end; two months suit it (`numberOfMonths={2}`). The calendar closes once both ends are chosen, and an end typed before the start is wrong.

<Stage path="date-picker/range" label="Range" />

## Presets

`presets` puts the ranges people choose most beside the calendar: Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days and This month, counted from today. A preset sets the range and closes the calendar.

<Stage path="date-picker/presets" label="Presets" />

## With a range

`defaultValue` is `[start, end]` as ISO dates; the matching preset shows as chosen.

<Stage path="date-picker/presets-chosen" label="With a range" />

## Your own presets

`presets` also takes the ranges your product reports on, by name.

<Stage path="date-picker/presets-custom" label="Your own presets" />

## A range's limits

`min`, `minDays` and `maxDays` bound the range; `startName` and `endName` send its ends under names of their own.

<Stage path="date-picker/range-limits" label="A range's limits" />

## In another language

`locale` (or the I18nProvider's) sets how the dates are written; the I18nProvider's `datePicker` and `calendar` words translate what it says.

<Stage path="date-picker/range-locale" label="In another language" />

## In a form

In a Field, the FieldLabel names the date and the FieldDescription and FieldError describe it. `required` keeps the form from being sent without a date, as text that is no date does; a form reset brings back `defaultValue`.

<Stage path="date-picker/form" label="In a form" />

## Sizes

32, 36 or 44px tall, the same as Input and Button.

<Stage path="date-picker/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## Limits

`min`, `max` and `unavailable` keep days from being chosen.

<Stage path="date-picker/limits" label="Limits" />
