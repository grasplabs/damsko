## Alone

Its icon is a child, `<Icon icon={BoldIcon} />`; an icon alone carries its name in `aria-label`, and shows it in a tooltip of its own under a resting pointer and on keyboard focus. `tooltip` gives other words, `tooltip={false}` none; a ToggleGroupItem takes the same.

<Stage path="toggle/alone" label="Alone" />

## With words

The words say what it turns on.

<Stage path="toggle/words" label="With words" />

## Outline

`variant="outline"` draws a field's hairline round it, for a toggle that must read as one before it is pressed.

<Stage path="toggle/outline" label="Outline" />

## A group

ToggleGroupItems on one track, one pressed at a time, raised out of it: the track of Tabs' default look. A ToggleGroup sets a value and shows nothing of its own; where each choice shows its own panel, use [Tabs](/components/tabs).

<Stage path="toggle/group" label="A group" />

## Several at once

`multiple` lets each item in the group be pressed on its own.

<Stage path="toggle/multiple" label="Several at once" />

## Full width

`fullWidth` lays the track across the width it stands in, the items sharing it: each grows from its words' width, and where the width is short their words end in an ellipsis rather than run past the track.

<Stage path="toggle/full-width" label="Full width" />

## Sizes

Button's ladder: `xs` 28px alone or in a toolbar, and the default; `sm` 32, `default` 36 and `lg` 44 beside a field of that size.

<Stage path="toggle/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## Disabled

Half strength; a group stays reachable by the keyboard.

<Stage path="toggle/disabled" label="Disabled" />

## Every state

Hover and keyboard focus are forced from the toggle's own CSS; the rest are its props.

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="toggle"
  always={[
    "A choice that stays on, such as a view, a text style or a filter over a list.",
    "Words that say what it turns on, the same whether it is on or off.",
    "An `aria-label` on a toggle that shows only an icon.",
    "A group name that says what its toggles set: View, Text style.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "An action that happens once: use a Button.",
    "In a form, where it sends nothing: use a Checkbox.",
    "A setting named by a label beside it: use a Switch.",
    "Switching between panels, each choice with content of its own: use Tabs.",
  ]}
/>
