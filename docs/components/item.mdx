## Basic usage

A name and a line about it, `outline` or on the `muted` fill.

<Stage path="item/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## With an icon

`ItemMedia variant="icon"` sets an icon in a square, which says what kind of thing it is.

<Stage path="item/icon" label="With an icon" />

## With actions

Its own buttons at the end, for what is done to this one thing.

<Stage path="item/actions" label="With actions" />

## A link or a choice with actions

A row with `href`, `onClick` or `asChild` may still hold `ItemActions`, wherever they stand among its parts: its title is the press, stretched over the whole row, and each action stands above it as its own stop for the keyboard, never inside the link.

<Stage path="item/link-actions" label="A link or a choice with actions" />

## Actions on hover

`ItemActions showOnHover` keeps the actions quiet until the row is pointed at or holds focus, and always shows them on touch and for the keyboard: a side panel's card of tools, one row each. In a SidePanelSection's card an `xs` row takes the panel's rhythm: 44px, the panel's smaller words, its bare icon in the accent, its `ItemDetail` at the row's end, and the list flush with a hairline between rows. The row that adds one more is an Item with `onClick`, after the list.

<Stage path="item/tools" label="Actions on hover" />

## A person or a provider

An Avatar or a logo in `ItemMedia align="center"` stands in the middle of its row. In a narrow row the actions drop under the words.

<Stage path="item/members" label="A person or a provider" />

## Among setting rows

`size="xs"` stands a list on the setting rows' height and inset, so a settings page has one rhythm.

<Stage path="item/rhythm" label="Among setting rows" />

## A quieter detail

`ItemDetail` puts a word after the name on its line, in the muted ink: a role, or "You".

<Stage path="item/detail" label="A quieter detail" />

## An icon in line

The default `ItemMedia` stands an icon bare on the title's line, with no square: for a dense row.

<Stage path="item/inline-icon" label="An icon in line" />

## As links

With `href` the row is one link: its title is the link, stretched over the whole row, named by the title and described by the description. `separated` on the ItemGroup draws the page's hairline between every two rows; an `ItemSeparator` draws one between two.

<Stage path="item/links" label="As links" />

## With no chevron

Where every row of a list plainly opens its own page (a page's own sections, a list of sources), `showChevron={false}` leaves the link's chevron out.

<Stage path="item/sections" label="With no chevron" />

## A router's link, the open one

`asChild` makes a router's own link the row's press, drawn round the title and stretched over the row; `active` marks the open one of a list beside its detail.

<Stage path="item/router" live label="A router's link, the open one" />

## As a choice

`onClick` makes the row one button, its title stretched over the whole row, named by the title and described by the description: a provider to choose. No chevron, since it goes nowhere.

<Stage path="item/choice" label="As a choice" />

## Out of use

`disabled` puts a link or a button row out of use: dimmed as a disabled Button is, said as unavailable, and it takes no press. Say why near it, in its description, which is said with its name.

<Stage path="item/disabled" label="Out of use" />

## Loading

Before the data, `loading` keeps every row's boxes: each part given empty waits as a quiet placeholder of its own size, an ItemMedia with no mark its square or its icon's box, an ItemTitle and an ItemDescription their lines.

<Stage path="item/loading" label="Loading" />

## As tiles

`ItemGrid` lays small outlined Items out as tiles, as many per row as fit (each at least 15rem), the row's tiles one height. It is a list, read row by row, as an app's tiles on an Integrations page.

<Stage path="item/grid" label="As tiles" />

## Sizes

16px of room by default, 12px above and below in a dense list.

<Stage path="item/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## Header and footer

`ItemHeader` and `ItemFooter` are lines of their own above and under the row's parts.

<Stage path="item/header-footer" label="Header and footer" />

## Every state

Only a row with a press (`href`, `onClick`, `asChild`) has states: hover, keyboard focus and pressed, forced from its own CSS.
