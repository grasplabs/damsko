## A row of suggestions

`Suggestions` holds the row; each `Suggestion` is a button that hands its `suggestion` to `onClick`, to send it or to put it in the prompt.

<Stage path="suggestion/basic" label="A row of suggestions" />

## Above the prompt input

Put the row above the Prompt input. The words are the message: send them as they are.

<Stage path="suggestion/prompt" label="Above the prompt input" />

## More than fit

The row never wraps. What does not fit runs past its end and scrolls sideways by wheel, touch and Tab, with no bar; the side with more beyond it fades out.

<Stage path="suggestion/overflow" label="More than fit" />

## One longer than the row

A suggestion is never wider than its row: one too long for it stays on one line and ends in an ellipsis, and keeps every word as its name.

<Stage path="suggestion/long" label="One longer than the row" />
