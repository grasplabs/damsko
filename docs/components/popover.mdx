## With a title and description

A title that names it, a sentence under it, and the action that finishes it. The trigger and Cancel are plain wrappers: `asChild` hands their behaviour to a Button. The action is a Button whose `onClick` returns a Promise and closes the popover once its work is done.

<Stage path="popover/basic" live label="With a title and description" />

## Only words

A popover that only explains holds no control, so focus stays on its trigger as it opens: no ring frames the panel, and Escape or a press outside closes it. With a control in it, the first takes focus. `initialFocus` changes either.

<Stage path="popover/explains" live label="Only words" />

## Long content

When it holds more than it shows, its body scrolls.

<Stage path="popover/long" label="Long content" />

## Placement and alignment

`side` picks where it opens, and `align` lines it up with the trigger.

<Stage path="popover/placement" label="Placement and alignment" />

## With a tooltip

An icon-only trigger can say what its popover is for with a Tooltip round it. The tooltip never shows while the popover is open.

<Stage path="popover/tooltip" label="With a tooltip" />

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="popover"
  always={[
    "A title that says what the popover is for, in sentence case: Publish this report.",
    "Cancel first, then the one action the popover is for, last.",
    "An `errorLabel` on the action's Button that says plainly what did not happen: Could not publish.",
  ]}
  never={["A popover opened from inside another popover."]}
/>
