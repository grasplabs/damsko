## The set

Each mark is the brand's own, filled and in its own colours. Damsko draws none: the product keeps its brands' logos, each an `IconData` made with `filledMark` from lib/icons, and passes one as `logo` with the brand's name as `label`. Only `label` is required: without `logo` the tile shows the first letter, `tile` is on unless turned off, and `decorative` is off unless the name is in the words beside it. A black or white mark, such as GitHub's or X's, draws its parts in `currentColor`, so it follows the ink and stands in both themes.

<Stage path="brand-icon/set" label="The set" />

## Sizes

Avatar's sizes on a tile, `xs` 24px to `lg` 48px, and the mark alone (`tile={false}`) for beside words.

<Stage path="brand-icon/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## Variants

`brand`, the default, draws the brand's colours; `mono` draws the same shape in the text colour, where colour would be too loud.

<Stage path="brand-icon/tones" label="Variants" />

## In a list

When the brand's name is in the words beside it, the mark is `decorative`.

<Stage path="brand-icon/sources" label="In a list" />

## A group

`BrandIconGroup`: a few brands as one row of tiles, then "+N" for the rest, named once ("Slack, Google Drive, Notion and 2 others"), as Avatar group does. The tiles stand side by side, a step apart.

<Stage path="brand-icon/group" label="A group" />

## A brand with no mark

`label` with no `logo`: the tile shows the brand's first letter on its quiet ground, in the same squircle as the marks beside it, never Avatar's circle. The name is the tile's name for a screen reader.
