## Basic usage

`min` and `max` keep it in range: a number typed past an end is brought to it when the field is left.

<Stage path="number-field/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## A unit or a sign

`suffix` after the number, `prefix` before it. `step` sets one step, `largeStep` what Page Up and Down move, `smallStep` what Alt with an arrow moves.

<Stage path="number-field/units" label="A unit or a sign" />

## The page's language

Separators follow the page's language, or `locale`. A form gets the plain number.

<Stage path="number-field/locale" label="The page's language" />

## Sizes

32, 36 and 44px, as Input's, so the two stand level in a row.

<Stage path="number-field/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## States

Invalid with its reason from the Field, read only, and disabled.
