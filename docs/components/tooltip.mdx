## A hint for a button

A few words that add to what the button says. The trigger is a plain wrapper: `asChild` hands its behaviour to the Button inside.

<Stage path="tooltip/basic" label="A hint for a button" />

## Placement

`side` picks the side of the trigger it opens on.

<Stage path="tooltip/placement" label="Placement" />

## Alignment

`align` lines up a tooltip wider than its trigger.

<Stage path="tooltip/align" label="Alignment" />

## With a shortcut

`shortcut` names the keys on the trigger; a `Kbd` in the content shows them, written for the platform with `useShortcutHint`: ⌘S on a Mac, Ctrl+S elsewhere. The words never break to make room for the keys: when both do not fit on one line, the keys stand under the words, at the line's end.

<Stage path="tooltip/shortcut" label="With a shortcut" />

## Long text

Longer words wrap onto more lines, and the corner stays round.

<Stage path="tooltip/long" label="Long text" />

## Delay

`delay` sets how long the pointer rests before it opens; a `TooltipProvider` sets it for every tooltip inside.

<Stage path="tooltip/delay" label="Delay" />

## On an icon-only button

An icon-only Button, Toggle or ToggleGroupItem shows its `aria-label` in a tooltip by itself, and takes other words by its `tooltip` prop, as SidebarMenuButton does. Wrap one in a Tooltip only to say more than its name: the button keeps its `aria-label` as its name, and your tooltip stands in place of its own and adds to it.

<Stage path="tooltip/icon" label="On an icon-only button" />

## On a link or the host's own element

With `asChild` the trigger is a router's own link, or any element the host draws (a face, a logo): it keeps its own look and name, and the tooltip is its description.

<Stage path="tooltip/link" label="On a link or the host's own element" />
