## Basic usage

A short title, one sentence on what will appear and when, and an icon if it says it faster.

<Stage path="empty/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Without a title

In a card or one tab of a list, the sentence alone says it.

<Stage path="empty/no-title" label="Without a title" />

## Sizes

`lg` stands for a whole list, `default` for a card or a tab, `sm` for a small box.

<Stage path="empty/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## A picture of the case

EmptyMedia's `illustration` tells the cases apart before a word is read. The title still says it.

<Stage path="empty/pictures" label="A picture of the case" />

## At the head of the place

`align="start"` stands the words where a list's first row will land.

<Stage path="empty/top" label="At the head of the place" />

## With an action

`EmptyContent` puts the one way on under the sentence.

<Stage path="empty/action" label="With an action" />

## While it is set up

EmptyMedia holds a part of its own, such as the Working mark, in the icon's place while the place is set up.

<Stage path="empty/setting-up" label="While it is set up" />

## When it did not load

`variant="destructive"`, for when the request failed. Its words are said out loud, not its actions; `announce={false}` on a route's error page.

<Stage path="empty/error" label="When it did not load" />

## As the page's title

EmptyTitle's `level` makes the title a heading, same look: `1` when the state is the whole page.

<Stage path="empty/page-title" label="As the page's title" />

## Every state

A sentence or a title alone, at the `sm` size. Failed has its own section above, since its icon always shows.

<States

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="empty"
  always={[
    "A list with nothing in it yet: say what will appear, and when.",
    "A list whose request failed.",
    "A title only where the state stands alone: a page or a whole list.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "A list that is still loading: use Skeleton.",
    "One part of a loaded page failed: use Alert.",
    "An action failed: the control says so itself.",
    "Jokes or blame: Oops, nothing here!",
    "The same words as the title in the sentence under it.",
  ]}
/>
