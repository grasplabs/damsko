## In a sentence

Rest on the name, or Tab to it and wait; a press still follows the link.

<Stage path="hover-card/person" label="In a sentence" />

## In a list

`align="start"` lines the card up with the list it opens from.

<Stage path="hover-card/document" label="In a list" />

## Through a router

`asChild` puts the trigger on the router's own link (React Router's or TanStack's `Link`), as Tooltip's trigger takes it: one link, with the trigger's look and the card on it.

<Stage path="hover-card/router" label="Through a router" />

## On a control

`variant="default"` with `asChild` leaves the trigger in the look of the element inside: a Button, a Badge, an avatar. It adds no underline, colour, weight or focus ring of its own.

<Stage path="hover-card/control" label="On a control" />

## Sized to its content

`width="content"` makes the card as wide as what it holds, from hover-card-min-width to hover-card-max-width, and never wider than the window less the gutter.

<Stage path="hover-card/fit" label="Sized to its content" />

## Opened from outside

Pass `open` with `onOpenChange` to open it from elsewhere: here a Button shows the card, and moving off it, Escape or a press outside close it as usual.

<Stage path="hover-card/controlled" label="Opened from outside" />

## Every state

Hover and keyboard focus are forced from the link's own CSS; the card waits for its delay.
