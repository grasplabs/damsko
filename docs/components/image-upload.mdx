## In a setting's row

`ImageUpload` is one picture, the picture itself the control: a square well with soft corners showing it, or its `fallback` (initials) while there is none. Press it or drop a picture on it to change it; the remove stands at its corner on hover, on focus and always on touch. What it takes is in its tooltip, not a line of text.

A file it refuses is said with why, under the well; in a Field's row without the file's name, as the person has just picked it.

<Stage path="image-upload/setting" label="In a setting's row" />

## Large, and saving

`size="lg"` stands on a page of its own. Given `onUpload`, the well shows the save: the new picture dimmed with a spinner, then kept, or a destructive edge with Try again. A failed save keeps the new picture under Try again, so trying again is plain; remove puts the old one back.
