## With a label

A checkbox always has its words beside it, saying what checking it does. The checkbox draws its box only; in a Field with `orientation="horizontal"`, the Checkbox first and its FieldLabel after it, a press on the box, its label or the space around them checks it.

<Stage path="checkbox/label" live label="With a label" />

## With a description

Add one sentence under the label when the label alone leaves a question.

<Stage path="checkbox/description" label="With a description" />

## In a group

Choices that make one answer share a `CheckboxGroup` in a Field, whose FieldLabel names the group; each box stands in its own horizontal Field. A `parent` box checks them all, and shows a dash while only some are (`checked="indeterminate"` on a box your own code holds); `nested` moves a box in under the parent's words.

<Stage path="checkbox/group" live label="In a group" />

## Disabled

It keeps its state in view, and the sentence beside it says why it cannot change.

<Stage path="checkbox/disabled" label="Disabled" />

## When it is wrong

The edge turns danger and the error says how to put it right, until the box is checked: `data-invalid` on the Field, or `aria-invalid` on the Checkbox itself, with a FieldError beside it.

<Stage path="checkbox/invalid" label="When it is wrong" />

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="checkbox"
  always={[
    "One choice on or off, or several that make one answer, in a form that waits for a Save button; a group of them with a name above it.",
    "Words that say what checking does: Send me a weekly summary, not Weekly summary?",
    "A choice someone must accept, like terms, with an error that says how to put it right: Accept the terms to start the export.",
    "Say why near a disabled checkbox.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "A choice that does something at once: use a Switch, or a Button.",
    "One answer from a few choices: use a Radio group.",
    "A negative label, such as Do not send me emails.",
    "A box without its words, or mint for a checked box: mint is for approving.",
  ]}
/>
