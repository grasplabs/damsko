## Steps

`Trace` is a list of lines. A `TraceLine` straight inside it is a step: a quiet line of dots down from its mark to the next step's, its `TraceTitle` and a `TraceDescription`. In every line a first `TraceIcon` sits in a seat at its start, so titles start at one edge. A `TraceIcon` with nothing in it is the status mark.

<Stage path="trace/steps" label="Steps" />

## A row that opens

Put the `TraceLine` in a `TraceCollapsibleTrigger` and the detail in `TraceCollapsibleContent`: a quiet row, as wide as its line, its words muted until the pointer is on it or it is open, its chevron turning as it opens. More lines in a `Trace` inside it make a chain of thought.

<Stage path="trace/chain-of-thought" label="A row that opens" />

## A tool call

A line in a trigger can hold more than its title: here a Badge says the call's state. The chevron stands straight after the line.

<Stage path="trace/tool" label="A tool call" />

## Thinking

The thoughts open under a row that says how long the thinking took.

<Stage path="trace/reasoning" label="Thinking" />

## A commit

A row with a line for each file the commit changed: what changed in the title, the lines in the description, the diff under each.

<Stage path="trace/commit" label="A commit" />

## A plan

When every step is known before the work starts, show them all from the start: the ones not begun are `pending`. The row's line holds the goal as its title and the progress as its description, and is `running` while the plan is worked through.

<Stage path="trace/plan" label="A plan" />

## While it works

A line is said once, when its status changes after it was drawn: when it finishes or fails. A line is never said for appearing. Press Next to finish the running step.

  always={[

    "A line in a trigger to make it open, or in a link or a dialog's trigger to make it go somewhere.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "A step a part built on Trace already names: use Tool for a call, Reasoning for thinking, Chain of thought for an answer's steps, Queue or Subagent.",
    "The assistant's reply: use Message.",
    "A question for the person, such as approving a tool: use Confirmation, outside the Trace.",
  ]}
