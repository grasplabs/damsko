## While the assistant writes

`MessageScrollerProvider` holds where the transcript stands; with `autoScroll` it stays at the end as the answer arrives, gliding a frame at a time as the words come. Scroll up, or press a key up, and it stays put, and `MessageScrollerButton` shows the way back to the latest. Only the person lets go: a scroll the browser makes on its own does not, and while the answer streams neither does one set from code; move the log from code with `useMessageScroller`. Wrap each row in a `MessageScrollerItem` with its `key` and `messageId`.

<Stage path="message-scroller/live" label="While the assistant writes" />

## Room for the answer

Mark the person's own turns with `scrollAnchor`. A turn sent after it opened goes to the top, below the top's fade, and the answer grows into the room under it; once the answer is taller than that room, it follows the end. `scrollPreviousItemPeek` sets how much of the row before stays in view.

<Stage path="message-scroller/answer" label="Room for the answer" />

## Opening a saved chat

With `defaultScrollPosition="last-anchor"` a saved chat reopens at its last question with the answer under it, rather than at the very bottom. `end`, the default, stands at the latest before scripts run; `start` opens at the first.

<Stage path="message-scroller/opening" label="Opening a saved chat" />

## Jumping to a message

`useMessageScroller` drives the transcript from anywhere inside the provider: `scrollToMessage`, `scrollToEnd` and `scrollToStart`. `useMessageScrollerVisibility` says which turn is being read, and `useMessageScrollerScrollable` which ways there is more. `direction="start"` puts a way to the first message over the log.

<Stage path="message-scroller/jump" label="Jumping to a message" />

## Loading earlier messages

Rows drawn above the ones you are reading leave what you read where it is (`preserveScrollOnPrepend`), and are not counted as new.

<Stage path="message-scroller/history" label="Loading earlier messages" />

A log that follows its end (`autoScroll`) holds the row being read too, once the reader has scrolled away from the end, at the very top of the log as well; it does not go back to the end.

<Stage
  path="message-scroller/history-following"
  label="Loading earlier messages in a log that follows its end"
/>

## A long conversation

Every row is drawn; the browser skips the ones out of view.

<Stage path="message-scroller/long" label="A long conversation" />

## Loading an old chat

With `loading`, the log is busy and says so once; draw placeholder rows meanwhile. The turns then come in the same place, at the latest.

<Stage path="message-scroller/loading" label="Loading an old chat" />

## Named by its heading

`aria-labelledby` points at the chat's own heading, so its name is written once. Given neither name, the log is called Messages.

<Stage path="message-scroller/named" label="Named by its heading" />
