## Vertical

Give it a height; what runs past it scrolls.

<Stage path="scroll-area/vertical" label="Vertical" />

## Horizontal

`orientation="horizontal"` for a row wider than the box.

<Stage path="scroll-area/horizontal" label="Horizontal" />

## Words alone

With nothing inside to focus, `aria-label` makes it a named tab stop the keyboard can scroll.

<Stage path="scroll-area/words" label="Words alone" />

## Every state

The bar shows while the pointer is over it; thumb hover and keyboard focus are forced from its own CSS.
