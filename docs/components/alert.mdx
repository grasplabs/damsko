## Variants

`variant` says what the note means: `default` something to know, `success` it went well, `warning` something to look at, `destructive` it went wrong, `info` a plain note.

<Stage path="alert/variants" label="Variants" />

## Compositions

Every part is optional: an `AlertDescription` alone, an `AlertTitle` alone, or no icon.

<Stage path="alert/compositions" label="Compositions" />

## Banner and subtle

`appearance="banner"` is news about the whole page or the app, across its top: its `AlertAction` after the words, and with `onClose` a close button at the end. `appearance="subtle"` drops the fill and the inset, for an alert inside a row or a rail.

<Stage path="alert/banner" label="Banner and subtle" />

## How it is heard

`live` sets it in a live region's words: polite, assertive or off. It wins over the variant's own role.

<Stage path="alert/live" label="How it is heard" />

An alert is read, not pressed: its states are its five variants and its parts.

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="alert"
  always={[
    "Something to know, something done or something wrong, near what it is about.",
    "Say what it means and what to do, in one or two sentences.",
    "A title only when the sentence needs a few words before it.",
    "One alert per message, near what it is about.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "A receipt that something happened, for a moment: use Toast.",
    "What is wrong with one field of a form: use Field.",
    "Two alerts saying the same thing.",
    "Colour alone: the icon and the words say it too.",
  ]}
/>
