## Basic usage

A title, a sentence under it, and the actions that finish the task. The trigger is a plain wrapper: `asChild` hands its behaviour to a Button. Cancel is a `DrawerClose`, which draws an outline Button beside the action; the action is a `default` Button whose `onClick` returns a Promise and closes the drawer once its work is done. While it works, the drawer holds: its `onOpenChange` cancels the close.

## Two heights

`snapPoints` lets it rest low or high. A drag or a press on the handle moves it.

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="drawer"
  always={[
    "A title that names the task, a verb first.",
    "A panel on a phone that rests at more than one height, or that a person drags: results under a map, a player, a list to pull up.",
    "`snapPoints` when the lower height is useful on its own, as a list of results.",
    "`dismissible={false}` while a drag would lose unsaved work: ask in `onDismissAttempt`.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "A short task on a phone with nothing to drag: use Dialog, which becomes a sheet along the bottom under 640px by itself.",
    "A task on a wide screen: use Dialog or Sheet.",
    "A second task over an open drawer: finish one first.",
  ]}
/>
