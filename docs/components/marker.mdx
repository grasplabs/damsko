## Between messages

`Marker` holds a `MarkerIcon` and `MarkerContent`: something that happened to the conversation itself, or a word between two rules. It stands between Messages. A step an assistant takes, or its work under way, is a [Trace](/components/trace) line.

<Stage path="marker/demo" label="Between messages" />

## Variants

`variant`: `default` a line of words, `separator` its words in the middle of a hairline, `border` a line with a hairline under it.

<Stage path="marker/variants" label="Variants" />

## Separators

For a day, where the unread messages start, or a span of work. Its words are ordinary content: give it no `separator` role, which would hide them. With a Button beside its words it is a checkpoint, a point the conversation can go back to: [Chat](/components/chat) shows one.

<Stage path="marker/separator" label="Separators" />

## A status

With `role="status"` its words are said as they change: the conversation reconnecting, someone typing. Put a Spinner in its MarkerIcon, or its words in a Shimmer. An assistant's work under way is not a Marker: a [Trace](/components/trace) line with `status="running"` shimmers its title and says the line once when it ends.

## Links and buttons

With `asChild`, the Marker draws the one link or button it is given: underlined, reached by Tab, named by its words.

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="marker"
  always={[
    "A line that marks something about the conversation itself: a day, New messages, someone joined, a checkpoint.",
    '`role="status"` on a line whose words change while it stands (Reconnecting), so they are said as they change.',
    "Words beside an icon: the icon is hidden from screen readers.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "A message: use Message, with a Bubble for its surface.",
    'A step an assistant took, or its work under way: use a Trace line (`status="running"` while it works).',
    'A `role="separator"` on a separator with words: its words would not be read.',
    "An icon alone with no name: give it words or an `aria-label`.",
  ]}
/>
