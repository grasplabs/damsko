## Tokens used

`ContextTrigger` is an outline Button, a quiet trigger, with a ring of how full the window is and its share in words. Resting on it, or tabbing to it, opens `ContextContent`: `ContextContentHeader` with the tokens used of the most and a bar of the same, and `ContextContentBody` with a row per kind of token. A row with none of its kind draws nothing.

<Stage path="context/basic" label="Tokens used" />

## What it cost

Context works out no price and knows no model. Give `cost` the amounts the product worked out, in their `currency`: each row shows its kind's, and `ContextContentFooter` the total.

<Stage path="context/cost" label="What it cost" />

## In the page's language

Every number is formatted with `Intl.NumberFormat` in the I18nProvider's `locale`: tokens compact, the share whole, the cost in its currency.
