## Tab A: moves on

The default: Tab steps cell to cell, as a spreadsheet's does. To leave sooner, press Ctrl or Command with End, then Tab.

<Stage path="data-grid/tab-move" label="Tab A: moves on" />

## Tab B: leaves

With `tabNavigation="leave"` the grid is one stop: Tab leaves at once, and the arrows alone move inside.

<Stage path="data-grid/tab-leave" label="Tab B: leaves" />

## Rows that change

Rows can come and go while the grid is open, as a refetch brings them. The chosen cells and an open editor keep to their rows, by `getRowId`; an editor whose row goes closes, and focus stays in the grid.

<Stage path="data-grid/live" label="Rows that change" />

## Read only

Without `onCellsChange` the grid is read-only: cells are chosen and copied, and a paste changes nothing.

<Stage path="data-grid/read-only" label="Read only" />
