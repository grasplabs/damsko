## When to use it

- Between groups of content that belong apart, where space alone does not divide them.
- Not where space alone already divides, above a heading that already starts a group, or after another separator. A line people drag to resize what is on each side is a Resizable.
- Between the rows of a list, never after the last one.
- `decorative` when the words around it already divide, such as "or" between two ways to sign in: a screen reader skips it.
