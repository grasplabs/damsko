## Basic usage

Fields down one column, two in a row where they belong together, the buttons at the end.

<Stage path="form/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Sections

Fields under a title and a sentence, with a hairline between sections.

<Stage path="form/groups" label="Sections" />

<Part title="Using it" />

## Required fields

A required control's label shows a quiet mark, drawn beside its words: the control's name stays the label's words alone.

<Stage path="form/required-names" label="Required fields" />

## An error of your own

Set `data-invalid` only when it is true; leave it out otherwise, since `data-invalid={false}` hides the browser's own check.
