## Variants

`soft` is the usual tag; `outline` is quieter, beside tags that are chosen.

<Stage path="tag/variants" label="Variants" />

## Sizes

`default`, 24px, beside body text; `sm`, 20px, in a dense row or a table.

<Stage path="tag/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## With an icon

An icon before the words says what kind of label it is.

<Stage path="tag/icon" label="With an icon" />

## Removable

`onRemove` adds a small button after the words that takes the tag away.

<Stage path="tag/removable" label="Removable" />

## Every state

A tag has nothing to press; its remove button's hover and keyboard focus are forced from its own CSS.
