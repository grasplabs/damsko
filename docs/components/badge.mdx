## Variants

Five variants are a status, how something stands, and the words say it too: `default`, `success`, `warning`, `destructive` and `info`.

<Stage path="badge/tones" label="Variants" />

## Labels

Two variants are a label, what sorts or names something ("Design", "Q3 plan"): `secondary` is the usual label; `outline` is quieter, beside labels that are chosen. A label says nothing about how the thing stands, so it has no colour of its own.

<Stage path="badge/labels" label="Labels" />

## Sizes

22px beside a title or in a list; 18px in a dense table or a row of small text.

<Stage path="badge/sizes" label="Sizes" />

A label is a step taller, with its words a size up: `default`, 24px, beside body text; `sm`, 20px, in a dense row or a table.

<Stage path="badge/label-sizes" label="A label's sizes" />

## With an icon

An icon before the words makes a status quicker to find in a long list.

<Stage path="badge/icon" label="With an icon" />

On a label, the icon says what kind of label it is.

<Stage path="badge/label-icon" label="A label with an icon" />

## Removable

`onRemove` adds a small button after the words that takes the badge away, as a filter or a chosen value does. Only a label takes it (`secondary`, `outline`): a status is not something a person takes away, and the type refuses it.

<Stage path="badge/removable" label="Removable" />

## In a row

A badge stands at the end of the row it describes, after the thing's name.

<Stage path="badge/in-a-row" label="In a row" />

## With its meaning on focus

`tooltip` says what a status means, for whoever wonders: the badge becomes a stop for the keyboard and shows the words in a tooltip on hover and on focus, said as its description. It still looks like a badge, and nothing happens when it is pressed.

<Stage path="badge/explained" label="With its meaning on focus" />

## Working on it

`loading` turns a mark in the icon's place while something happens: an invitation sending.

<Stage path="badge/pending" label="Working on it" />

## How serious, as a dot

`dot` is how serious something is, as a small dot in the variant's colour beside words in the text's colour, for a page full of them, such as captured logs. Alone, the dot is named by its variant (`badge.dot`).

<Stage path="badge/dot" label="How serious, as a dot" />

## As a link

`asChild` draws a link as the badge: it stays a link, named by its words, with the keyboard's ring. A label drawn as a link is cut with an ellipsis as any label is, its whole text in its `title`. Its words are its own, so `icon` and `loading` are not drawn: put an `<Icon />` in the link before its words.

<Stage path="badge/link" label="As a link" />

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="badge"
  always={[
    "How something stands, in a word or two, beside what it describes: `default`, `success`, `warning`, `destructive` or `info`.",
    "What sorts or names something (Design, Q3 plan): `secondary`, or `outline` beside chosen ones.",
    "A word or two in sentence case: Needs review, not NEEDS REVIEW.",
    "The same variant for the same status everywhere in the product.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "A status variant for a label, or a label variant for a status.",
    "Words that a label cuts off: say it shorter.",
    "Something to press: use Button; a link to follow goes in `asChild`.",
    "Mint for a badge: mint is for approving.",
    "Colour as the only difference between two statuses.",
  ]}
