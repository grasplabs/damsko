## Variants

Each variant is a status, and the words say it too: `default`, `success`, `warning`, `destructive` and `info`.

<Stage path="badge/tones" label="Variants" />

## Sizes

22px beside a title or in a list; 18px in a dense table or a row of small text.

<Stage path="badge/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## With an icon

An icon before the words makes a status quicker to find in a long list.

<Stage path="badge/icon" label="With an icon" />

## In a row

A badge stands at the end of the row it describes, after the thing's name.

<Stage path="badge/in-a-row" label="In a row" />

## With its meaning on focus

`tooltip` says what a status means, for whoever wonders: the badge becomes a stop for the keyboard and shows the words in a tooltip on hover and on focus, said as its description. It still looks like a badge, and nothing happens when it is pressed.

<Stage path="badge/explained" label="With its meaning on focus" />

## Working on it

`loading` turns a mark in the icon's place while something happens: an invitation sending.

<Stage path="badge/pending" label="Working on it" />

## How serious, as a dot

`dot` is how serious something is, as a small dot in the variant's colour beside words in the text's colour, for a page full of them, such as captured logs. Alone, the dot is named by its variant (`badge.dot`).

<Stage path="badge/dot" label="How serious, as a dot" />

## As a link

`asChild` draws a link as the badge: it stays a link, named by its words, with the keyboard's ring. Its words are its own, so `icon` and `loading` are not drawn: put an `<Icon />` in the link before its words.

<Stage path="badge/link" label="As a link" />
