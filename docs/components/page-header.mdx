## Basic usage

The page's name in the serif, one line under it, and the actions at the end, the main one last.

<Stage path="page-header/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## With tabs

A page with views carries its tabs under the title.

<Stage path="page-header/tabs" label="With tabs" />

## A long description

`descriptionLineClamp` cuts a description someone wrote short after one or two lines. Its title keeps the whole text.

<Stage path="page-header/long-description" label="A long description" />

## Loading

`loading` puts placeholders where the words will be. `loadingLines` reserves the lines a wrapping description will take.

<Stage path="page-header/loading" label="Loading" />

## Without a shell

In an app shell the crumbs stand in its page bar. A page without one may put a Breadcrumb above the title, and a Badge beside it (`badge`).

<Stage path="page-header/breadcrumb" label="Without a shell" />

## A settings page

`size="sm"` for a settings page: the serif at the middle size, no lead, as dense as a sub-page.

<Stage path="page-header/settings-page" label="A settings page" />

## A sub-page

`size="xs"` for a sub-page whose crumbs say where you are: the title a step down, in the sans, no lead.

<Stage path="page-header/sub-page" label="A sub-page" />

<Part title="Using it" />

## Actions on a phone

On a phone the last Button stays at the title's end; the others fold into More, a destructive one last. Narrowest, the main one is its icon alone, named by its words.

<Stage path="page-header/actions" label="Actions on a phone" />

## Every kind of action, folded

A disabled, working (a Spinner in it) or link (`asChild` with an `<a href>`) Button folds as that same kind of item.

<Stage path="page-header/actions-kinds" label="Every kind of action, folded" />

## A banner

`variant="banner"` heads a record's or a note's page: flush under the bar, a faint dither, and one line drawn along its foot. `accentColor` lights the dither in the colour of the page's object, and `media` puts its mark over the title.

<Stage path="page-header/band" label="A banner" />

Outside a shell it stays where it is, its own room round its words.

<Stage path="page-header/band-long" label="A banner outside a shell" />

## A toolbar

`toolbar` takes a `PageHeaderToolbar`, the row under the title that changes the list below: the kinds to browse at its start, whose they are and a search at its end. When the row is narrow, the two stack.

<Stage path="page-header/toolbar" label="A toolbar" />

## A search and an action

With nothing to browse, the search takes the toolbar's start and the page's action its end.

<Stage path="page-header/toolbar-actions" label="A search and an action" />
