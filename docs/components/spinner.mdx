## Sizes

`default` beside small text and in a button, `lg` beside body text, `xl` alone in a panel.

<Stage path="spinner/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## Beside words

Words beside it say what is working and are the status; it takes their colour.

<Stage path="spinner/with-words" label="Beside words" />

## Alone

Alone in a panel that is still loading, with an `aria-label` a screen reader says.

<Stage path="spinner/alone" label="Alone" />
