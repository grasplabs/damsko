## With a label

The switch draws itself only; its words are Field's parts. In a Field with `orientation="horizontal"`, the FieldLabel first and the Switch at the end: the label names the setting and stays the same, on or off, and a press on the switch, its label or the space around them turns it. A switch has one size.

<Stage path="switch/label" label="With a label" />

## With a description

One sentence under the label, a FieldDescription in a FieldContent with the label, says what turning it on does.

<Stage path="switch/description" label="With a description" />

## Pressing and dragging

Pressed, the thumb stretches towards where it will go. Drag it across and let go: the side it is on decides.

<Stage path="switch/press" live label="Pressing and dragging" />

## Saving as it turns

With `onSave`, it holds still with a spinner while it saves (`loading`), and says so when it could not (`error`): its `errorLabel` ("Could not save" unless you give your own) shows in the row, under the words.

<Stage path="switch/save" live label="Saving as it turns" />

## In a setting row

In a responsive Field the switch stands on the group's end edge, named and described by the Field's words. Outside any Field, give it an `aria-label` where the words around it already name it.

<Stage path="field/legend" label="In a setting row" />

## Disabled

It keeps its setting in view, and the sentence beside it says why it cannot change.

<Stage path="switch/disabled" label="Disabled" />
