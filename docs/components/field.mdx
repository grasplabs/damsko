## Basic usage

The label, the control, a line of help and the error, one under the other. The label shows the required mark when its control is `required`; the error shows once a send finds the control wrong.

<Stage path="field/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## An error from your code

`data-invalid` shows the field as wrong at once, for an error your code decides: the server said no.

<Stage path="field/invalid" label="An error from your code" />

## A control and its words on one row

`orientation="horizontal"`: a Checkbox or a Radio first and its words after it, or the words first and a Switch at the end. FieldContent holds the label and its description.

<Stage path="field/horizontal" label="A control and its words on one row" />

## Settings

`orientation="responsive"`: a setting, its name and a line of help at the start and its control on an end edge every Field in its FieldGroup shares, a hairline between them. A field's description keeps no word alone on its last line; a setting's help wraps as it falls, as the original's setting row did. In a flush Card each topic is a card of rows. A control of ours inside needs no ids and no spread: the Field wires its label and help to it.

<Stage path="field/responsive" label="Settings" />

Under 34rem of its own width, a row puts its control under its words.

<Stage path="field/narrow" label="In a narrow card" />

## Named groups

A FieldSet with a `FieldLegend variant="label"` names its rows for a screen reader and the eye, on a page with no card. A set after another stands under a hairline, so its name belongs to the rows under it. `controls="fit"` on a FieldGroup stands each control at its own width, beside its words at any width.

<Stage path="field/legend" label="On a page, no card" />

<Stage path="field/sets" label="Two sets in one card" />

## Saving as it changes

`FieldStatus` shows the save beside the control: a Spinner while it saves (`loading`), then a tick that leaves after a moment (`success`). Nothing under the name changes and the row keeps its size. Only a failure (`error`) writes under the name: it keeps the value and offers Try again (`onRetry`). Each is said once, politely; the words are the I18nProvider's (`field.statusLoading`, `field.statusSuccess`, `field.statusError`, `field.statusRetry`).

<Stage path="field/status" label="Saving as it changes" />

## Sets of fields

`FieldSet` and `FieldLegend` for fields that belong together, a FieldDescription right inside the set to describe it, a FieldGroup to stack the fields and a FieldSeparator between parts of one form.

<Stage path="field/set" label="Sets of fields" />

## Out of use

`disabled` dims the control, its label and its description together. Say why in the description.

<Stage path="field/disabled" label="Out of use" />

## Focus

A field you type in shows focus by its own edge: the hairline goes a step darker (3:1 or more on the page) and a faint halo in the brand's mint stands round it, red when the field is wrong. There is no ring and no dark fill: the caret already says where the words go. A press and the keyboard look the same. Buttons and every other control keep the focus ring.

<Stage
  path="field/focus"
  label="Every text field, at rest, wrong and out of use"
/>

Every size and variant takes the same focus. Select's `text` variant is a ghost button in a sentence, so it keeps the button's ring.

<Stage path="field/focus-sizes" label="Every text field at every size" />

## Every control it names

Each control in a Field takes its name from the FieldLabel, required marks and all.
