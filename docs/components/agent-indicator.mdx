## Beside words

Nine square pixels, the light passing through them, its core in the accent. Words beside it say what is working and are the status.

<Stage path="agent-indicator/with-words" label="Beside words" />

## Alone

Alone, with an `aria-label` a screen reader says.

<Stage path="agent-indicator/alone" label="Alone" />

## Named later

Work may start before its name is known. Without a name it says nothing; once the `aria-label` comes, it is a status that says it, and keeps moving.

<Stage path="agent-indicator/named-later" label="Named later" />

## Success

With `success`, it stops and a tick is drawn in where it stood. Leave it out where a place has its own mark for it.

<Stage path="agent-indicator/done" label="Success" />

## Every state

The dither glyph's three paths, the three marks it was chosen from (`variant`), and success.

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="agent-indicator"
  always={[
    "While work really runs, beside words that say what it is: Searching the web.",

  never={[
    "A second one in the same view.",
