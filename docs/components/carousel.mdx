## Basic usage

One slide a view, with Previous, Next and a mark for each page.

<Stage path="carousel/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Several a view

Three slides a view, going round from the last page to the first.

<Stage path="carousel/several" label="Several a view" />

## Down

`orientation="vertical"` runs the slides down: the up and down arrows page them, and Previous and Next point up and down. Give the content a height.

<Stage path="carousel/vertical" label="Down" />

## In another language

Its words come from the `I18nProvider`: the buttons, the row of marks and each mark, what a screen reader calls it and its slides (`carousel.carousel`, `carousel.slide`), and the name of a slide that has none (`carousel.slideLabel`, "2 of 5").

<Stage path="carousel/labels" label="In another language" />
