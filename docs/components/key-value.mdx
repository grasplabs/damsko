## Basic usage

Each name in a few words, its value under it.

## Divided, with copy

A line between the rows for a list on its own. An id goes in the mono face; `copyable` puts a Copy button at its row's end.

## In another language

The words the list says itself come from the page's `I18nProvider`: the copy button's (`copyButton`) and what a waiting value says (`keyValue.loading`).

<Stage path="key-value/labels" label="In another language" />

## Horizontal

`orientation="horizontal"` for a short list in a small space; a long value drops under its name.

<Stage path="key-value/inline" label="Horizontal" />

## Facts that open

Horizontal, a row with a `detail` opens in place, as a [Collapsible](/components/collapsible) does: its name is a button over the whole row, its detail opens under it over its height, one row is open at a time, and only the rows below it move. A `group` starts after a little room. A `loading` value waits as Skeleton's dot field at its own width, then crossfades in.

<Stage path="key-value/rail" label="Facts that open" />

`valueAlign="start"` sets every value on one column just after the widest name, close to it, with the chevrons at the row's end. A row that does not open keeps the chevron's room, so every value ends on one line. A row whose value is still on its way does not open until it lands, and nothing moves when it does.

<Stage path="key-value/rail-start" label="Values on one column" />

## With icons

An `icon` stands before its name in the name's muted ink, on a column of its own; a row without one keeps its room. For a tight rail, `labelDisplay="icon"` shows the icons alone: each names its row for a screen reader, and in a Tooltip on hover and focus.
