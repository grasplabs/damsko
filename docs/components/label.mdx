## Basic usage

`htmlFor` ties the name to its control by the control's `id`: a press on the name puts the caret in the field. Inside a [Field](/components/field), use FieldLabel, which is this label wired for you.

<Stage path="label/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Required

`required` adds a quiet mark after the words when the control it names is required. The mark is drawn by the label, never part of its text.
