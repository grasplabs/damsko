## Approve or decline

Built on an outlined small [Item](/components/item): the title its ItemTitle, the answers its ItemActions, beside the title while there is room. The request, what it touches, and the two answers. An answer works in place until its Promise settles, keeping its width (Button follows the Promise); then the title says what was decided, the answers go, and focus that was on one lands on the title.

<Stage path="confirmation/basic" label="Approve or decline" />

## By state

It draws what its `state` and `approval` say, as the AI SDK gives a tool's: the answers while approval is requested, the decision once answered, and nothing while the input still comes. It does not run an answer as work in place, ask for changes, offer Undo or count down an expiry: an approval card that does is the product's, built from these parts.

<Stage path="confirmation/states" label="By state" />

## A grave yes

With `hold`, the yes is held, or confirmed once more after a tap.

<Stage path="confirmation/grave" label="A grave yes" />

## An answer that fails

An answer whose Promise rejects says so on its own button, and the request still waits.

<Stage path="confirmation/failed" label="An answer that fails" />

A held answer that fails says so on its own button too, and the next hold sends it again.

<Stage path="confirmation/held-failed" label="A held answer that fails" />
