## Basic

Its items, a separator, and one item out of use. The trigger is any control, most often a Button given with `asChild`. `DropdownMenuMore` is the whole thing for a row's actions: a ghost icon Button with an ellipsis, named by its `aria-label`, and its menu drawn from `items`.

## With icons and shortcuts

An icon before the words, and a DropdownMenuShortcut at the end with the keys that do the same.

<Stage path="dropdown-menu/icons" label="With icons and shortcuts" />

## Groups and labels

A label names each group, and separators set them apart.

<Stage path="dropdown-menu/groups" label="Groups and labels" />

## With a chevron

A chevron after the trigger's words says a menu opens from it.

<Stage path="dropdown-menu/chevron" label="With a chevron" />

## A long name on one line

In a narrow head a long name would wrap and make the head taller. The trigger's words end in an ellipsis on one line, and its `aria-label` keeps the whole name.

<Stage path="dropdown-menu/truncate" label="A long name on one line" />

## Checkbox and radio items

Options that stay set: a tick marks what is on, a dot the chosen one, and the menu stays open. The page holds each choice (`checked` with `onCheckedChange`, `value` with `onValueChange`): a menu's items are taken out when it closes, so the menu keeps none itself.

<Stage path="dropdown-menu/choices" label="Checkbox and radio items" />

Two items may say the same words: each is its own, told apart by its group's label, and turns over alone.

<Stage path="dropdown-menu/same-words" label="The same words twice" />

## A destructive item

`variant="destructive"` draws it in red and an agent asks first; an `onSelect` that returns a Promise shows its work.

<Stage path="dropdown-menu/danger" label="A destructive item" />

## Submenus

A DropdownMenuSub holds the actions that share one verb; Right opens it and Left closes it (the other way round in right-to-left text). Its first item stands level with the item that opened it, and its words start past an icon only when one of its own items has one. It opens beside its parent on the side with room, on the other side when only that one has room, and over its parent when neither has (on a phone), always whole on the screen and scrolling when it is taller than the window. Over its parent, a tap on the parent's rows still showing goes back one level, as Escape does; so does a tap anywhere else outside it when a touch or a pen opened the submenu, while after the mouse or the keys a press there closes the whole menu.

<Stage path="dropdown-menu/submenu" label="Submenus" />

## Items that are links

With `asChild`, an item is the link it holds, an `<a href>` or a router's own link: a press, Enter, a middle click or a new tab opens it, and the menu closes as it goes.

<Stage path="dropdown-menu/links" live label="Items that are links" />

## Side and alignment

`side` picks where it opens, and `align` lines it up with the trigger.

<Stage path="dropdown-menu/placement" label="Side and alignment" />

## Every state

The trigger is a Button: hover, keyboard focus and pressed are forced from its own CSS; disabled is its prop.
