## Basic

A title and one sentence that says what the section holds.

<Stage path="section-header/basic" label="Basic" />

## In a section

`Section` keeps the same room between the header and each part under it.

<Stage path="section-header/section" label="In a section" />

## Loading

`loading` puts placeholders where the words will be.

<Stage path="section-header/loading" label="Loading" />

## Beside a long title

The action stays on the title's row while it fits; a long title wraps beside it.

<Stage path="section-header/actions" label="Beside a long title" />

## Over a card

`variant="group"` names a group of rows standing over its card: the title a step over the rows' own labels, and an xs action raised so its foot meets the foot of the title's line. Compose it with a Card in a `section` named by the header's `id`.

<Stage path="card/grouped" label="Over a card" />

## Every state

A header has nothing to hover or press: it rests or loads. Its actions keep their own states.

<States
  path="section-header/states"
  column
  cells={[{ label: "Rest" }, { label: "Loading", props: { loading: true } }]}
/>
