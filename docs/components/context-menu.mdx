## Actions

Icons lead the words, a shortcut sits at the end, and an action that destroys is in red.

<Stage path="context-menu/file" live label="Actions" />

## Settings

Checkboxes turn a setting on or off; a radio group chooses one.

<Stage path="context-menu/view" label="Settings" />

## Submenus

A ContextMenuSub holds the actions that share one verb; Right opens it and Left closes it (the other way round in right-to-left text). It opens beside its parent on the side with room, on the other side when only that one has room, and over its parent when neither has (on a phone), always whole on the screen and scrolling when it is taller than the window.

<Stage path="context-menu/submenu" label="Submenus" />

<Part title="Using it" />

## Round a link

With a link inside that takes the keys, the trigger's `tabStop={false}` leaves the tab stop to it, and the content's `finalFocus` gives focus back.

## Inside a dialog or a sheet

An area inside a Dialog, a Sheet or a Popover opens its menu the same ways. On a touch screen a long press opens it, and lifting the finger leaves it open.

<Stage path="context-menu/in-dialog" label="Inside a dialog" />
