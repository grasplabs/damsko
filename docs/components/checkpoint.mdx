## After a turn

`Checkpoint` is a separator Marker after a turn: its words in the middle of the hairline, and a `CheckpointTrigger` beside them. Restore keeps that turn and drops every message after it. Name the trigger for its checkpoint (`aria-label`) and say in its `tooltip` what restoring does; with nothing after it, put it out of use and say why.

<Stage path="checkpoint/demo" label="After a turn" />

## With a mark

`CheckpointIcon` puts a bookmark before the words, or an `<Icon />` of its own. Without a `tooltip` the trigger is a plain button.
