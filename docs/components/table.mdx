## Basic usage

A head that names the columns, a row header that names each row, and a caption under it.

## Sortable columns

You own the sort: pass `sortDirection` and `onSort`, and the head says the sort to a screen reader.

<Stage path="table/sortable" label="Sortable columns" />

## With a footer

A footer on the soft ground holds a total for the rows above.

<Stage path="table/footer" label="With a footer" />

## Selected rows

A `selected` row keeps a quiet fill, and says so in its words too.

<Stage path="table/chosen" label="Selected rows" />

## Wider than its column

A wide table scrolls sideways in its own box, which then takes keyboard focus. A cell or a head with `wrap` lets its words run onto more lines, keeping room for a short word, so a table of sentences fits a narrow column and scrolls only when its columns cannot shrink further; `streaming` shares the width evenly and keeps each column a few words wide. `dir="auto"` on a head or a cell lets words in Arabic or Hebrew stand at its right and read from there.

<Stage path="table/wide" label="Wider than its column" />
