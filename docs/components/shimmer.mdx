## While it works

Muted words, and a narrow band of light crossing them. It runs only while the work does.

<Stage path="shimmer/basic" label="While it works" />

## Variants

`soft`, `default` and `bright`, on a short line and a long one: they sweep at the same apparent speed.

## One shimmer

The light needs a second copy of the words to travel under, so it is a component, not a class: wrap the words in `<Shimmer>`. TraceLine's `status="running"` does it for its title. There is one implementation: no part keeps a shimmer of its own.

## Reduced motion and forced colours

- With reduced motion there is no sweep: the words say it. In forced colours it is plain words.
- A screen reader reads the words once: the light's copy of them is hidden from it.
