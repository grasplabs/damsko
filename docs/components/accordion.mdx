## Basic usage

One section open at a time: opening one closes the other.

<Stage path="accordion/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Several open

`multiple` lets sections stay open side by side. A section can be disabled.

<Stage path="accordion/multiple" label="Several open" />

## Every state

One section's heading through its states, then open and disabled.
