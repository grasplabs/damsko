## Basic

Place one `Toaster`, then call `toast()` when something finishes.

<Stage path="toast/basic" label="Basic" />

## One per page

Put one `<Toaster />` at the page's root, outside every layer: it draws the shared `toast`, from anywhere on the page, a Data table's included. Two on the same toasts show each one twice, and a screen reader hears it twice, so a second one says so in the console. A part with a queue of its own (`createToaster()`) gives its own Toaster that queue.

## Types

Success, warning, error and info each have an icon in their colour; the default has none.

<Stage path="toast/types" label="Types" />

## Duration

3.5 seconds, 8 with an action, or `duration` in milliseconds. A pointer, focus or a hidden tab pauses it.

<Stage path="toast/duration" label="Duration" />

## With actions

An action such as Undo stands under the words. A Promise keeps the toast while it runs, and a Spinner shows in the action; if it fails, the action says `toast.error` (Could not finish), once to a screen reader, and the toast stays.

<Stage path="toast/actions" label="With actions" />

## How a toast goes

A toast goes by time, swipe, Escape or its action, and fades as it goes. Only one that stays has a close button.

<Stage path="toast/dismiss" label="How a toast goes" />

## Over a dialog

A toast stands over every layer: pressing it, or its action, while a Dialog, Alert dialog, Sheet, Drawer or Popover is open does not count as a press outside, so the layer stays and Undo runs. From the keyboard, F6 reaches the toast from inside the layer, and Tab its action.

<Stage path="toast/in-dialog" live label="Over a dialog" />

## In the middle

`position="top-center"` for a message about the whole page. The default, `bottom-right`, stands beside the work; `left` and `right` follow the reading direction.

<Stage path="toast/center" label="In the middle" />

## Update by its id

`toast.update(id, …)` changes a toast in place: a loading toast becomes its result. `data.icon` gives one toast its own icon.

<Stage path="toast/update" label="Update by its id" />

## API

`toast()` and its `.success`, `.warning`, `.error`, `.info`, `.loading` and `.promise` show a toast: 3.5 seconds, or 8 with an action. `toast.dismiss()` closes.
