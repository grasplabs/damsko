## To-dos

`Queue` is a Trace with a `QueueSection` for each part of the list, a row that opens, open at first. `QueueSectionLabel` is its line: the count and what it holds. `QueueList` holds the `QueueItem` lines, each a `QueueItemIndicator` and its `QueueItemContent`; a done to-do's circle is ticked, and a screen reader hears it is done.

<Stage path="queue/basic" label="To-dos" />

## More to say

A short note is a `QueueItemDescription` after the words. A longer one opens under the to-do: put its `QueueItem` in a Trace row that opens.

<Stage path="queue/descriptions" label="More to say" />

## Messages sent ahead

`QueueItemActions` stand at the end of the item's line; each `QueueItemAction` is a small ghost button named by its `aria-label`. The product keeps the queue: here Send now and Remove take a message out of it. `QueueItemAttachment` holds the files a message carries, in a row under its words: `QueueItemImage` as a small preview, `QueueItemFile` by its name. An image's address comes from a model or a person, so it loads only from http, https, a path on this site, a picture written into the address (`data:image/png;base64,…`) or one the page made (`blob:`); any other is not drawn.

<Stage path="queue/messages" label="Messages sent ahead" />

## Pictures from a chat

A `QueueItemImage` draws only from an address that cannot tell another site who looked: one written into the address (`data:` of a picture type, never SVG), one the page made (`blob:`) or a path on this site; an `http(s)` address draws only with `allowRemote`, for a source you trust.

<Stage path="queue/untrusted" label="Pictures from a chat" />

## While it works

The to-do in progress runs and the ones after it wait (`pending`); once done it stays on its line, ticked, and is said once. Press Next to finish it.
