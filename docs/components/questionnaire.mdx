It is a group, not a form, so it can stand inside a form of its own, a PromptInput's.

## One choice

A `QuestionnaireItem` holds a `QuestionnaireTitle`, a `QuestionnaireDescription` and its `QuestionnaireChoices`: radios. Each choice is a card with the key that picks it boxed at its start. A `QuestionnaireInput` after them is one more card for an answer of the person's own (Other), which takes the chosen choice's place. Send waits for an answer; once sent, the answer stands in one line. On an item that is not `required`, `QuestionnaireSkip` skips it; here its `onClick` lets the product move on.

<Stage path="questionnaire/single" label="One choice" />

## Several choices

`multiple` on the item: the choices are checkboxes.

<Stage path="questionnaire/multi" label="Several choices" />

## Two columns

`columns={2}` on `QuestionnaireChoices`, for short choices with no description; one column on a phone.

<Stage path="questionnaire/columns" label="Two columns" />

## The person's own words

A `QuestionnaireInput` alone in its item, named by the title. Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a line.

<Stage path="questionnaire/text" label="The person's own words" />

## Several questions

One item shows at a time, in the page's order. `QuestionnaireProgress` says where the person is; `QuestionnaireNext` waits for an answer and goes on, `QuestionnairePrevious` goes back with the answer kept, and `QuestionnaireSubmit` on the last item sends every answer. `items` gives the order before the items are drawn, so the server's first draw shows the right one.

<Stage path="questionnaire/steps" label="Several questions" />

## An answer that fails

When `onSubmit`'s Promise rejects, the questionnaire says it could not send, keeps the answer, and can be sent again.

<Stage path="questionnaire/failed" label="An answer that fails" />

## Held by the page

`checked` on a `QuestionnaireChoice` and `value` on a `QuestionnaireInput` hold the answer in the page; `onCheckedChange` and `onValueChange` say each change.

<Stage path="questionnaire/controlled" label="Held by the page" />

## Names from a model

Item names a model writes are keys of the answers like any other, even those every object knows (`__proto__`, `constructor`, `toString`).

<Stage path="questionnaire/untrusted" label="Names from a model" />

## In a prompt input

A questionnaire is a group, not a form, so it may stand in a PromptInput's body for a while. Enter on a choice and its Send answer the question; the message is not sent.

<Stage path="questionnaire/in-prompt-input" label="In a prompt input" />

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="questionnaire"
  always={[
    "Questions an assistant needs answered before it goes on: a choice, several, or a few words, one question at a time.",
    "Mark the choice the assistant recommends with `recommended`, one at most.",
    "Say in the title or description that a `required` question must be answered: nothing else shows it.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "A yes before a step the assistant takes: use Confirmation.",
    "A few prompts the person may send next with one press: use Suggestion.",
    "A form the person fills in on their own: use Field and Form.",
  ]}
