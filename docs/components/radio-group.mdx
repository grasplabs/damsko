## In a group

The group's name asks the question, and each radio's label is one answer to it. The RadioGroup stands in a Field, whose FieldLabel names it; each RadioGroupItem draws its circle only, in its own horizontal Field with its FieldLabel, and a press on the circle, its label or the space around them chooses it. Tab lands on the chosen radio and the arrow keys move the choice, the other way round in right to left.

<Stage path="radio-group/basic" live label="In a group" />

## With descriptions

A sentence under the group, or under a choice, says what choosing it means.

<Stage path="radio-group/description" label="With descriptions" />

## Disabled

The group keeps its choice in view, and the sentence under its name says why it cannot change.

<Stage path="radio-group/disabled" label="Disabled" />

## When it is wrong

The edges turn danger and the error says what to do, until one is chosen: `data-invalid` on the Field round the group, with its FieldError. A `required` group shows the same after a send with nothing chosen.

<Stage path="radio-group/invalid" label="When it is wrong" />

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="radio-group"
  always={[
    "One answer from a few choices, all in view at once, under a group name that asks the question: Summary email, then Daily, Weekly, Never.",
    "A choice already made when one is safe, so nobody has to choose before they can go on.",
    "Say why near a disabled group.",
    "An error that says how to put it right: Choose a file type to start the export.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "More than about seven choices: use a Select.",
