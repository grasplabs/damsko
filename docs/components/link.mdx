## In a sentence

It takes the sentence's colour and size, and its underline finds it without colour.

<Stage path="link/inline" label="In a sentence" />

## On its own

`variant="plain"`: foreground and medium, underlined under the pointer, for a link on its own line, such as View all reports.

<Stage path="link/standalone" label="On its own" />

## Leaving the product

`external` opens it in a new tab, with a mark after the words and the same said to a screen reader.

<Stage path="link/external" label="Leaving the product" />

## With a router

Give the router's own link as the child, with `asChild`: it becomes the element, with the look, its own words and props, and no wrapper. Link knows no router.

<Stage path="link/router" label="With a router" />
