## Actions

Groups name a run of actions; typing ranks them, and a shortcut runs one from anywhere on the page. Its parts: CommandInput, CommandEmpty and CommandList. A CommandList with no children draws the root's `items`; or write the list out with CommandGroup, CommandItem, CommandShortcut and CommandSeparator.

## Work that takes time

A command shows it is working, then says so on the command if it failed.

<Stage path="command/working" label="Work that takes time" />

## Search results

The page owns the words and the results: it fetches as the words change, the list says it is searching meanwhile, and shows what comes back in the page's order. A result can carry a second line, and something of the page's at its end.

<Stage path="command/search" label="Search results" />

## People, with their faces

`media` puts a face or a mark where a row's icon would stand: an Avatar or a Brand icon.

<Stage path="command/people" label="People, with their faces" />

## More for a result

Enter runs a result; a quiet More at its end holds what else it can do. It shows on the highlighted or hovered row, and always on a touch screen. The right arrow opens it from the keys; Left or Escape comes back. Right to left, the two arrows swap.

<Stage path="command/actions" label="More for a result" />

## A palette over the page

CommandDialog puts the list in a dialog's panel over the page: its button opens it, and so does its `shortcut` (mod+k, ⌘K on a Mac and Ctrl+K elsewhere) from anywhere. A shortcut without mod, Ctrl or Alt (`/`) waits while someone types in a field, and no shortcut opens a palette over a dialog already open. A command run closes it, and focus goes back to what opened it. Held by the page (`open`, `onOpenChange`, `trigger={null}`), the app's own button, a Sidebar row or a menu opens it. It takes what Command takes for a server's search and a row's actions (`inputValue`, `onInputValueChange`, `filter`, `loading`, `onItemAction`), and a command that returns `false` keeps it open. `current` marks the row that is open now, and CommandHighlight marks the words a search found in a row's second line.

<Stage path="command/dialog" live label="A palette over the page" />

## Written out by hand

The list as parts: CommandGroup with its `heading`, CommandItem with the command's words as children, its keys as a CommandShortcut and what `items` says of a command as props, and CommandSeparator. A CommandItem's own `onAction` runs before the Command's.

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="command"
  always={[
    "A person finds an action by typing and runs it.",
    "The list over the page, opened from a button or a shortcut: CommandDialog.",
    "`inputValue`, `onInputValueChange` and `filter={false}` for search results the page fetches itself, kept in its order.",
    "`actions` on a command, with `onItemAction`, when a result has more to do than its one action: behind a quiet More, and the right arrow.",
    "Commands that start with a verb: New project, not Project.",
    "Groups when there are more than a handful.",
    "An errorLabel for every command whose work can fail.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "A shortcut the browser already uses, such as Ctrl+J or Ctrl+N.",
    "Colours or corners set from outside: `style` takes layout and width only.",
    "A command that only goes to another place: a Link.",
    "Choosing a value in a form: a Combobox or a Select.",
  ]}
/>
