## Basic usage

What is under way in a few words, in a `ProgressLabel`, and how far it has come, in a `ProgressValue`, which shows the percentage, or what a function given as its children makes of it (`{(formatted, value) => …}`). Where the words beside it already say it, leave both out and name it with `aria-label`.

<Stage path="progress/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Sizes

4, 8 or 12px tall, for a row, a panel, or a bar that is the point of its panel.

<Stage path="progress/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## How far unknown

`value={null}` while it is under way but how far is not known: fine square pixels on the track, and our light passing along them.

<Stage path="progress/unknown" label="How far unknown" />

## Variants

`success` once it has worked, `warning` when it is slow, `destructive` when it stopped.

<Stage path="progress/tones" label="Variants" />

## Circle

`ProgressCircle`, the same drawn as a ring, beside words or in a button's place, named by `aria-label`: a ring shows no words. A large ring holds its value.

<Stage path="progress/rings" label="Circle" />

## Every state

From not started to done, how far unknown, each variant, as a bar and as a circle.
