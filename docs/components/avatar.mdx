## Sizes

40px unless the row is dense or a profile heads it. The initials stand at about half the size.

<Stage path="avatar/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## Picture or initials

The picture once it loads; the initials until then, without one, and when it fails.

<Stage path="avatar/image" label="Picture or initials" />

## Beside a name

Beside its own name it is decorative, so the name is said once.

<Stage path="avatar/beside-a-name" label="Beside a name" />

## A badge

`AvatarBadge` puts a small mark on the avatar's lower end corner, a dot or a small icon. Say what it means in words beside it too.

<Stage path="avatar/badge" label="A badge" />

## A group

`AvatarGroup`: a few people (`items`) as one stack of faces, up to four (`max`), then +N (`AvatarGroupCount`) for the rest. Each face is cut back where the next stands, so a gap of the ground shows between them on any ground. It is one picture to a screen reader, named once: "Anna de Vries, Ravi Menon, Sofia Laine, Tom Becker and 2 others", or its own `aria-label`. Both words come from the I18nProvider (`avatar.groupName`, `avatar.groupCount`).

<Stage path="avatar/group" label="A group" />

## Every state

A picture, initials from a name of one or more words, and a picture that cannot be read.

  of="avatar"
