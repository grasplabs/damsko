## Keys

A symbol carries its spoken name in `aria-label`.

<Stage path="kbd/keys" label="Keys" />

## A shortcut

A `KbdGroup` holds one key each, side by side, inside the sentence that says what it does.

<Stage path="kbd/shortcut" label="A shortcut" />

## In a tooltip

A `Kbd` inside a tooltip's content loses its key cap: no fill, edge or padding, its sign in the tooltip's quiet words at the end of the line (`tooltip-muted-foreground`), so a shortcut reads as part of the tip. See [Tooltip](/components/tooltip).
