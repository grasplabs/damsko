## Basic usage

SortableList holds the order; each SortableListItem is one item, and its SortableListHandle moves it. Two of Grasp's apps put a list in order this way: the admin, a company's onboarding steps; the web app, a field's options. Drag a handle, or focus one and press Space, the arrows, then Space. Keep the order in state: the new one comes back through `onValueChange` once the item is put down.

## Items with controls

Only the handle moves the item, so an item holds its own controls and they keep their keys and presses. `textValue` gives the item's name when its words say more, so a screen reader hears "Picked up Its AI, position 2 of 4".

<Stage path="sortable-list/controls" live label="Items with controls" />

## Items that are fields

A space typed in a field is a space; only a handle picks an item up.

<Stage path="sortable-list/fields" live label="Items that are fields" />

## Items that cannot be picked up

A `disabled` item cannot be picked up. It does not hold its place: the others move past it, and it moves aside for them. A `disabled` list moves nothing; say why near it.

## In a box that scrolls

Held near the top or bottom edge of the box the list stands in, or of the window, a drag scrolls it until the list's own end is in view; the item stays under the pointer and within the list.

<Stage path="sortable-list/scrolling" live label="In a box that scrolls" />

## Right to left

The handle stands at the line's start. Up and Down move an item in every language.

## When to use it

  of="sortable-list"
  always={[

  never={[
