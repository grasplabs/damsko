## Basic usage

Views of one thing, one panel shown at a time. The list is always a row. `variant="line"` on the list: a line under the chosen tab.

<Stage path="tabs/basic" live label="Basic usage" />

## Disabled and more than fit

Seven tabs in a narrow column, one of them out of use.

<Stage path="tabs/many" label="Disabled and more than fit" />

## Default

The default look: the chosen tab raised out of a soft track, the track a ToggleGroup stands on. Tabs switch panels: each tab shows a TabsContent. A control that sets a value and shows no panel of its own (a view mode, a text style) is a [ToggleGroup](/components/toggle).

<Stage path="tabs/segmented" label="Default" />

## Across its box

A `default` track is as wide as its tabs. With `fullWidth` it takes the whole width of its box (across a narrow column or a sidebar) and shares it equally between its tabs, each at least as wide as its words. `icon` puts an icon a step before a tab's words.

<Stage path="tabs/fill" label="Across its box" />

## As links

Views with their own address: links that look like tabs, the current one marked. A navigation, not a tablist: links go somewhere. With a router, its link is the child, with `asChild`.

<Stage path="tabs/links" label="As links" />

## Links in a narrow place

Where the links do not fit, they fold to one button with the current view; its menu holds the same links.

<Stage path="tabs/links-fold" label="Links in a narrow place" />

## Views with icons

An `icon` on each link, and `size="sm"` on the nav: the view you are on shows its icon and words, the others their icon alone, their words in a tooltip and still their name. The words ease in and out as the view changes.

<Stage path="tabs/views" label="Views with icons" />

## Tabs that close

`onClose` on a TabsTrigger, for open files: a small × at the tab's end, its own button named "Close" and the tab's words, and Delete on the focused tab. The chosen tab's × is the one Tab stop after it; focus moves to the tab beside one that closes. The × keeps its room, so no tab changes size under the pointer.

<Stage path="tabs/files" label="Tabs that close" />
