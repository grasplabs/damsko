## Basic usage

One date, chosen with a press or the arrow keys and Enter.

<Stage path="calendar/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Range

`mode="range"` chooses a start and an end; two months suit it (`numberOfMonths={2}`).

<Stage path="calendar/range" label="Range" />

## Several dates

`mode="multiple"` chooses any number of days.

<Stage path="calendar/multiple" label="Several dates" />

## Unavailable dates

`unavailable` and `min` keep days from being chosen; the keys still reach them.

<Stage path="calendar/unavailable" label="Unavailable dates" />

## Every state

Today, a range, a booked day struck through, days before `min` at half strength, and the days of the months round it, in one month. Hover a day, or reach it with the keys, for the rest.

<Stage path="calendar/days" label="Every state" />

## Language and week start

The month's and the weekdays' names, and the day the week starts on, come from the page's language: the I18nProvider's `locale`, else the page's `lang`, through `Intl`. `locale` and `weekStartsOn` set them for one calendar. Previous month and Next month are named by the I18nProvider (`calendar.previous`, `calendar.next`).

<Stage path="calendar/locale" label="Language and week start" />
