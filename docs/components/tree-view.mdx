## Files

Folders open and close; a press or Space chooses one item. A letter finds the next item.

<Stage path="tree-view/files" label="Files" />

## Several chosen

`selectionMode="multiple"`: Shift with the arrows, or a press with Ctrl or Command, chooses several.

<Stage path="tree-view/multiple" label="Several chosen" />

## Loaded when opened

`loadChildren` loads a branch the first time it opens; a spinner shows while it does.

<Stage path="tree-view/lazy" label="Loaded when opened" />

## Pages

`current` marks the open page and opens its branch; `onAction` goes there.

<Stage path="tree-view/pages" label="Pages" />

## The tree guide

`showIndentGuides` draws Sidebar's tree guide on nested items: a stem down from each open branch's chevron and a rounded elbow into each item, stopping at the last. Off unless given.

<Stage path="tree-view/guides" label="The tree guide" />

## Links, with counts and marks

A node's `href`, or `render` for a router's own link, makes its row a link (`current` marks the open one); `badge` puts a count or a mark at the row's end. The keys and typeahead stay the tree's: Enter follows the link, as a press does.

<Stage path="tree-view/links" live label="Links, with counts and marks" />

## A count, and a mark in a tone

`badge` is a node, so its tone is yours: a count stands as it is, in the row's muted tone, and a mark that asks for a look is wrapped in a span that gives it the warning colour. Wrapping it is the intended way; TreeView has no tone prop of its own.

<Stage path="tree-view/end" label="A count, and a mark in a tone" />

## Buttons on a row, and folders that only open

A node's `actions` are its row's buttons: small icon Buttons, each named for its row ("Rename notes.md"). They are drawn only for the row under the pointer and the row that holds focus, and on a touch screen for the rows in view, so a tree of thousands of rows mounts none of them; a long name may wrap as they come. Only the focused row's buttons are in the Tab order: Tab from a row goes to its buttons and then out of the tree, never through another row's. A press or a key on a button is the button's, so the row is not chosen or opened, and the buttons are no part of the row's name. When a button takes its own row out, focus goes to the row after it, else the one before. `selectable: false` makes a node one that opens and runs but is never chosen, as the folder here: the choice is always a file.

<Stage path="tree-view/actions" live label="Buttons on a row" />

## An icon per kind of file

`icon` is the node's own, so which file wears which icon is the product's table. Read the extension as an own key (`ownValue`): a file's name is data.

<Stage path="tree-view/file-icons" label="An icon per kind of file" />

## A filter over it

`filterable` puts a field over the tree. What is typed keeps the items whose words hold it, each with the folders above it opened, and marks the words as Command does; nothing matched says so. Escape empties it, and the folds come back as they were. Hold the words yourself with `filter` and `onFilterChange`.

<Stage path="tree-view/filter" label="A filter over it" />
