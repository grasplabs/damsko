## When it is done

Built as shadcn's Chain of Thought pattern is, on [Trace](/components/trace): `ChainOfThought` is a Trace with one row that opens, `ChainOfThoughtContent` a Trace of steps inside it, each `ChainOfThoughtStep` a line. `ChainOfThoughtHeader` holds the run's one line, which you write: what it did and how long it took. A press opens `ChainOfThoughtContent` and its steps.

<Stage path="chain-of-thought/basic" label="When it is done" />

## While it works

While a step is `active` the line runs and says that step, in the shimmer. A run that finishes while nobody has opened or closed it settles back into its one line. Press Play to run it.

<Stage path="chain-of-thought/live" live label="While it works" />

## Every status

A step is `pending`, `active`, `complete` or `error`, said in words as well as by its mark; `icon` gives it a mark of its own.

<Stage path="chain-of-thought/states" label="Every status" />

## Search results

A step holds what it found under its line: `ChainOfThoughtSearchResults`, a `ChainOfThoughtSearchResult` Badge for each site.

<Stage path="chain-of-thought/results" label="Search results" />

## A picture

A step that made or found a picture holds it under its line: `ChainOfThoughtImage`, with a quiet `caption`.

<Stage path="chain-of-thought/image" label="A picture" />
