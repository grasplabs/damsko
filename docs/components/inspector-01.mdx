## Basic usage

A head, then sections of rows. Press Add in Tools, then the bin on the row it brought. A block: it installs as source, to edit; for a layout it cannot say, compose Side panel yourself.

<Stage path="inspector-01/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Views

`views` are tabs in the head; each section names its view. Scroll the panel and they fold into the bar as icons.

<Stage path="inspector-01/views" label="Views" />

## Facts

A section's `facts` are a list of terms and values, a hairline between them. A missing value is a quiet dash, said Not set.

<Stage path="inspector-01/facts" label="Facts" />

## Empty

With no sections, `empty` says in one quiet sentence what will stand there.

<Stage path="inspector-01/empty" label="Empty" />
