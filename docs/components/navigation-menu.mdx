## Main links

Rest on a trigger or press it; more than three links stand in two columns.

<Stage path="navigation-menu/main" label="Main links" />

## Open on a press

`openOnHover={false}` for a row people pass over often, like an app's header.

<Stage path="navigation-menu/press" label="Open on a press" />

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="navigation-menu"
  always={[
    "A short line under each link that says what is there, not what it is called.",
    "The page someone is on marked with `active`, in the panel and on its trigger.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "A trigger that is also a link: its panel holds the links.",
    "More than about six items in the row.",
    "A panel of more than eight links: split it into two groups.",
    "Actions: use Menubar or Dropdown menu.",
    "The path back up to the page someone is on: use Breadcrumb.",
    "The places of a product, down its side: use Sidebar.",
    "Views of one thing: use Tabs.",
  ]}
/>
