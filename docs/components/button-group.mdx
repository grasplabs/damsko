## Basic usage

Actions on the same thing, with the words that say what each does. The buttons are plain `ghost` Buttons.

<Stage path="button-group/basic" label="Basic usage" />

## Icons

Icon-only buttons in a toolbar row; each label is its name.

<Stage path="button-group/icons" label="Icons" />

## Split button

The usual action, a hairline, and a button for its other ways.

<Stage path="button-group/split" label="Split button" />

## Sizes

Button's heights: 32px, 36px or 44px. The group's corner follows its buttons' size.

<Stage path="button-group/sizes" label="Sizes" />

## Vertical

Stacked, each button as wide as the widest.

<Stage path="button-group/vertical" label="Vertical" />

## Text

Quiet words at the buttons' height. With `asChild` they are the one element given, a Label here, which keeps its behaviour and takes the text's look.

<Stage path="button-group/text" label="Text" />

## Every state

Its buttons' states, in order. Hover, keyboard focus and pressed show on Send only, one button at a time.
