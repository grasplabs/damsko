## Default

Space, except on a button, or K plays and pauses while focus is in the bar. The arrows go back and on five seconds.

<Stage path="audio-player/basic" label="Default" />

## Narrow bars

The bar answers to its own width. The skips go first, then the times.

<Stage path="audio-player/narrow" label="Narrow bars" />

## Playback rates

The rate button steps through the `playbackRates` it is given, starting with the first.

<Stage path="audio-player/speeds" label="Playback rates" />

## A moment of the file

`startTime` and `endTime` play a part of the file. The page hears where it is with `onTimeUpdate` and seeks it through `ref`.

<Stage path="audio-player/moment" label="A moment of the file" />

## Failed

When the file cannot play, the bar says so in place of its controls.

<Stage path="audio-player/failed" label="Failed" />

## Sounds from a chat

A sound plays only from an address that cannot tell another site who listened: one written into the address (`data:audio/…`), one the page made (`blob:`) or a path on this site; an `http(s)` address plays only with `allowRemote`, for a source you trust, and any other says it cannot play.

<Stage path="audio-player/untrusted" label="Sounds from a chat" />

## In another language

Its words come from the `I18nProvider`: each button, the timeline's thumb, and where the sound is (`audioPlayer.timeOf`), in that language's order.

<Stage path="audio-player/labels" label="In another language" />
