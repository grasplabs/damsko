## Basic

One mark glides beside the section being read; the line under the title fills as you read. Tab stops once, arrows move within.

<Stage path="table-of-contents/basic" label="Basic" />

## Groups and collapsed sections

Sections with one `group` stand under its name; a `collapsed` one gets a chevron and a word for screen readers. Actions go in `footer`.

<Stage path="table-of-contents/parts" label="Groups and collapsed sections" />

## In a box that scrolls

For a page that scrolls in its own box, pass its selector as `container`. `offset` sets the reading line below a header that sticks.

<Stage path="table-of-contents/box" label="In a box that scrolls" />

## Compact

`variant="compact"` draws a tick per section, for a narrow column. Hover or focus opens the names; Escape closes them.

<Stage path="table-of-contents/compact" label="Compact" />
