## With a label

The `SliderLabel` says what it sets; the value stands at the end of the same line.

<Stage path="slider/basic" live label="With a label" />

## With a description

`format` writes the value as people read it, a percentage here; a sentence under it says what it changes.

<Stage path="slider/description" label="With a description" />

## A range

Two thumbs set the lowest and the highest; give each its name in `thumbLabels`, or the second goes unnamed.

<Stage path="slider/range" label="A range" />

## Disabled

It keeps its value in view, and the sentence under it says why it cannot change.

<Stage path="slider/disabled" label="Disabled" />

## In a Field

Without a `SliderLabel`, the Field round it names it with its FieldLabel and describes it with its FieldDescription, as a Switch or a RadioGroup stands.

<Stage path="slider/field" label="In a Field" />

## Upright

`orientation="vertical"` runs it from the bottom up; Arrow Up raises it. Its height comes from `style`.

<Stage path="slider/vertical" label="Upright" />

## Levels

`variant="levels"`: one of a short named scale, two to seven levels, chosen along a round track filled with a halftone of dots: dark up to the thumb, with a warm scatter at its edge, faint past it. The scale's ends are named under it; the chosen level is said in words to a screen reader.

<Stage path="slider/levels" label="Levels" />

### Every level

Low to Max. The top level is a state of its own: the dots take the working light, a wave runs along them, and the thumb glows.

<Stage path="slider/levels-sheet" label="Every level" />

### Sizes

`default`, a 32px track, on a page or in a form; `sm`, a 24px track, inside a popup or a menu.

<Stage path="slider/levels-sizes" label="Levels sizes" />

### Three levels

The same track with three stops. Under four levels the last is not extra; pass `top` to choose.

<Stage path="slider/levels-three" label="Three levels" />

### In a popup

The small size, beside other rows.

<Stage path="slider/levels-popup" label="In a popup" />

### Out of use

Half strength, no drag, no focus. Say why beside it.

<Stage path="slider/levels-disabled" label="Levels out of use" />

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="slider"
  always={[
    "A number along a scale, with its value in its own units, or a range: the lowest and the highest.",
    "A label that says what it sets, and the value in its own units.",
    "`levels`: how much of something in two to seven named steps whose order matters (effort, detail), lowest first, as a control of its own: a settings row, a popover.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "An exact number someone must type: use a NumberField or an Input.",
    "A few named choices with no order between them: use a RadioGroup or a Select.",
    "Effort chosen together with a model, in the model's own panel: that is ModelSelector's effort row, never a second control beside it.",
    "A setting that is on or off: use a Switch.",
    "A second readout of a level beside its heading: the track and its ends say it.",
  ]}
/>
