## A plan under way

`Plan` is a Trace with one row that opens. `PlanHeader` is its line: a `PlanTitle` with the goal and a `PlanDescription` that counts the steps done. `PlanContent` holds the steps, each a `TraceLine`: done, `running` or `pending`. With `isStreaming` the line runs.

<Stage path="plan/basic" label="A plan under way" />

## A word about the plan

A paragraph in `PlanContent` stands above the steps.

<Stage path="plan/description" label="A word about the plan" />

## An action and a footer

A button cannot sit in the line that opens the plan, so `PlanAction` stands beside it, at the end of the row. `PlanFooter` stands under the plan, open or closed.

<Stage path="plan/action" label="An action and a footer" />

## While it works

Each step that finishes is said once; the line runs until the plan is done. Press Next to finish the running step.

## When to use it

<Intent
  of="plan"
  always={[
    "Steps the assistant decides on before it starts, every one shown from the start, the ones not begun waiting.",
    "The goal as the title and the progress after it: Plan: add dark mode, 2 of 4 done.",
    "One plan per reply.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "Steps that come from the work itself, known only once the last is taken: use Task.",
    "What waits to be sent or done while an answer streams: use Queue.",
    "A button inside the plan's line: put it in PlanAction or PlanFooter.",
  ]}
/>
