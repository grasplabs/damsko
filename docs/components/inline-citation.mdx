## A source in the sentence

`InlineCitationText` holds the words the source supports; `InlineCitationCardTrigger` is a small secondary Button after them, named by where the source lives. Resting on it, or tabbing to it, opens `InlineCitationCardBody` with an `InlineCitationSource`: its title, a line about it, and a link to it by its host, in a new tab.

<Stage path="inline-citation/basic" label="A source in the sentence" />

## Several sources

The chip says the first source's host and how many more. In the card, `InlineCitationCarousel` pages through them one at a time; its header holds Previous, Next and which source of how many. `InlineCitationQuote` sets off words quoted from a source.

<Stage path="inline-citation/several" label="Several sources" />

## An address that leads nowhere

Every address is checked: only http, https and mailto become links, and the host shown is read from the checked address. Any other (`javascript:`, `data:`, a path) is shown as its words, and the chip says Source.

## When to use it

  of="inline-citation"
  always={[
    "A source beside the claim it supports, inside the answer's text.",

    "The addresses as the model or tool gave them: they are checked here.",
  ]}
  never={[
    "The list of every source an answer drew on: use Sources.",
    "A link that is the point of the sentence: use Link.",
  ]}
