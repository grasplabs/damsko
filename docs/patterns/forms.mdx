## What deletes it

What deletes the thing a form is about stands under the form, last on the page, in a section of its own that says what is lost: never in the form's row of actions, beside Save. A grave loss asks in an [Alert dialog](/components/alert-dialog); a small one that cannot be undone, such as a draft, takes a hold on the button; what can be undone does not ask at all, as [Confirmations](/patterns/confirmations) shows.
