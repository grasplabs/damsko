---
title: Confirmations
description: Ask first only when it cannot be undone; otherwise act and offer the way back.
---

<Head lead={frontmatter.description} />

## Can be undone

No question: it happens at once, and a toast says so with Undo.

<Stage path="patterns/confirmations-undo" label="Can be undone" />

## From a menu

Move to trash in a menu does not ask either: it happens as it is pressed, and a toast offers Undo.

<Stage path="patterns/confirmations-menu-undo" label="From a menu" />

## Cannot be undone

Hold to confirm; a click asks in a small popover, for anyone who cannot hold.

<Stage path="patterns/confirmations-hold" label="Cannot be undone" />

## When the loss is grave

An alert dialog names the thing and what is lost, and its action repeats the verb in danger.

<Stage path="patterns/confirmations-delete" label="When the loss is grave" />

## When it fails

Hold the button: it says plainly that the removal did not happen.

<Stage path="patterns/confirmations-failed" label="When it fails" />
