## Status and pages

Status is a badge at the end of its row; the page count sits under the table.

<Stage path="patterns/tables-status" label="Status and pages" />

## Search and no results

Search sits above the table it narrows; with no match, the table says so in its own body.

<Stage path="patterns/tables-search" label="Search and no results" />

## Choosing rows

Each row's checkbox is named for it; while rows are chosen, the bar counts them and acts on them.

<Stage path="patterns/tables-choose" label="Choosing rows" />

## On a phone

The row's name gives way first: it wraps in whole words and keeps 11rem. Every other cell stays on one line. When a row still does not fit, the table scrolls sideways inside its box, faded at the edge with more.
