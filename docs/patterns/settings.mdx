## The page

The title, then a heading over each group of rows, centred 640px wide (`Container size="sm" align="center"`). Each row is a responsive Field with one control at its own width (`FieldGroup controls="fit"`), so every row is one height.

<Stage path="patterns/settings-page" label="The page" />

## On a phone

The column takes the whole width. Each control keeps its own width beside its words, which wrap.

<Stage path="patterns/settings-page-phone" label="On a phone" />

## Members

People on the setting rows' height: a face, a quieter You and a role on one edge.

<Stage path="patterns/settings-members" label="Members" />

## Plain settings

What can change as a setting, what cannot as a read-only field. A failed save keeps the choice and offers Try again (`FieldStatus`).

Help under a label is one short phrase. Anything longer, a sentence or a line that would wrap, goes under the group's title.

<Stage path="patterns/settings-general" label="Plain settings" />

## One topic

A page with one topic is one card with no title of its own: the page's title names it.

<Stage path="patterns/settings-profile" label="One topic" />
