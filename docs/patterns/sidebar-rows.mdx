## Rename in place, and a row's menu

A row of a Sidebar is a link, so a press goes to its place. Two things are added round it, each from a part the library has.

- **Rename.** A double press on the row, F2 while it holds focus, or Rename in its menu puts an Inline edit in the row's place, held open by the page (`editing`). Enter keeps the name, Escape leaves it as it was, and leaving the field keeps it too. The row's icon stays where it was and the field stands a small step after it, as tall as the row, so the row does not jump. When the field closes, focus goes back to the row.
- **The row's menu.** A Context menu round the row opens on a right press, a long press on a touch screen, and the menu key or Shift+F10 while the row holds focus. `tabStop={false}`, as the link is the row's tab stop: the area is then no group of its own, and the row is read once, as its link.

## What it is made of

- **SidebarMenuItem** and **SidebarMenuButton** (`asChild`, the link) for the row at rest.
- **InlineEdit** with `editing` and `onEditingChange`, drawn in the row's place while it is renamed; `onSubmit` may return a Promise, and the field then waits for it and says how it went.
- **ContextMenu**, its trigger round the row named for the row (`aria-label`), its items the row's actions, Rename showing F2 in a **ContextMenuShortcut**.

A context menu cannot be found by looking. Where the actions must be seen, give the row a **SidebarMenuAction** that opens the same actions in a Dropdown menu, as [Sidebar](/components/sidebar#a-rows-actions) shows: it is always shown on a touch screen.
