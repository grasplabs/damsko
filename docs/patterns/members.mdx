## People

One list: a face, the email to copy, the role at the row's end with a line on what each may do, and a menu. Invited and removed are states, not tabs.

<Stage path="patterns/members-people" live label="People" />

## Invite

A dialog from the list's Invite button. The addresses are tags, so many can be pasted at once; the role they get, with its line; a team if any. Send invites checks the addresses first and marks a wrong one, then sends. If only some went, those leave the field, the rest stay marked with why, and the dialog stays open to try again.

Invite takes many addresses at once and checks them only when Send is pressed: each wrong one is marked, and said under the field. A send that went in part is never reported as done, or as failed: it says who was invited and who was not.

<Stage path="patterns/members-invite" live label="Invite" />

## Teams

One row a team: its name, how many people, their faces as an Avatar group, and a menu with Rename and Delete. The team's name opens it. Delete happens at once, with Undo in a toast.

<Stage path="patterns/members-teams" live label="Teams" />

## A team

The team's dialog, opened from the team's name: its name in a field, and one field to find people and tick them. Who is in the team shows ticked and first, so ticking never moves a row; unticking takes them out. The list scrolls inside the field, so the dialog keeps its size with forty people. Save changes keeps both, as Forms says.

<Stage path="patterns/members-team-dialog" live label="A team" />

## States

One person; forty people, paged twenty at a time, the search running over all of them; the people loading, each row a face and two lines; a team with no one in it, which says how to add people; and a team's save that fails, keeping its changes in the dialog with Could not save on its button.
