## The screen

The agent's request waits for your yes; Replay plays the run from the question, the agent working, and its answer with sources. [Open it on its own](/examples/patterns/agent-workspace) to try 1440, 1024 and 390.

<Stage path="patterns/agent-workspace" label="The screen" />

## What it is made of

The Sidebar in an App shell; a MessageScroller of Messages, the agent's holding its steps (ChainOfThought) and its Sources; a Confirmation waiting in the conversation; the PromptInput at the foot; and beside them, in the App shell's panel, the DiffViewer of what the agent asks to change. On a narrow window the panel is a Sheet, opened from the header or from See the change. The agent's thinking is left out: Reasoning is not in the library yet. Nothing on this screen was changed to make it fit.
