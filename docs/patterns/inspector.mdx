## A report's details

Owner, status, people with access, linked sources and activity. No views, so only the hairline comes as it scrolls. The inspector is the App shell's panel: beside the page on a wide window, a Sheet from the end on a narrow one. [Open it on its own](/examples/patterns/inspector-report) to try 1440 and 390.

## A person's record

Fields, tags, open approvals and notes, beside the list they came from; scroll and the views slide into the bar.
