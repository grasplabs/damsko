## The screen

Choose a request to read it beside the table and answer; [open it on its own](/examples/patterns/approvals-inbox) to try 1440, 1024 and 390.

<Stage path="patterns/approvals-inbox" label="The screen" />

## Nothing waiting

An empty tab says what will appear there, and when.

<Stage path="patterns/approvals-inbox-empty" label="Nothing waiting" />

## Loading

The table waits in its own loading rows, each column's placeholder on its column's side; the rows fade in where they stood, and nothing is counted until they are there.

<Stage path="patterns/approvals-inbox-loading" label="Loading" />

## When it fails

It says plainly that nothing loaded and nothing was answered, with a way to try again.
