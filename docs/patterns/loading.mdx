## Its own shape

When the layout is known, a dot field holds the place of the words, our light passing over it, so nothing moves when they arrive.

<Stage path="patterns/loading-skeleton" label="Its own shape" />

## A card whose words are coming

Card has no loading state of its own. While a card's title and sentence are still coming, a Skeleton stands in its CardHeader for each, as tall as the line it stands for (`size="lg"` for the title, `default` for the sentence), so the header keeps its height. The card says it is busy with `aria-busy`, and what it holds waits its own way.

<Stage path="patterns/loading-card" label="A card whose words are coming" />

## How far it has got

A long task of known length shows a bar that fills, and says when it is done.

<Stage path="patterns/loading-progress" label="How far it has got" />

## A short wait

When neither the length nor the shape is known, a spinner sits beside words that say what is loading.

<Stage path="patterns/loading-spinner" label="A short wait" />
